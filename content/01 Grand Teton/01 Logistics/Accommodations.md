# Accommodations — Grand Teton

## Pre/post climb (Jackson area)

- **Climber's Ranch** (American Alpine Club) — bunkhouse on-park, $40–50/night for AAC members. Climbing crowd, kitchen, showers. Reserve early summer for July–Aug. [AAC Climbers Ranch](https://americanalpineclub.org/climbers-ranch)
- **Gros Ventre Campground** — NPS, $35/night, mostly first-come-first-served, only ~15 min to Lupine Meadows. Hot in Aug.
- **Jackson hotels** — pricey ($250+ summer). Cheaper options in **Driggs, ID** over Teton Pass (~45 min) or **Victor, ID**.
- **Dispersed camping:** Bridger-Teton NF outside park — free, no facilities. Shadow Mountain area is popular.

## On the climb

- **Lower Saddle (11,600 ft)** — standard high camp. National Park backcountry permit required ([[Permits & Registration]]). Exum has a permanent yurt — only Exum clients (or guides) get inside.
- **The Meadows (9,200 ft)** — lower bivy option, more often used for descent / weather days.

## Booking timing

- AAC Climbers Ranch: reserves fill 2–4 weeks ahead in peak summer (Jul–Aug). 1 week is usually enough for late Aug / Sep.
- Gros Ventre: walk-up mostly; if booking, recreation.gov 6 months ahead
