# Travel & Flights — Grand Teton

Home airport is **DEN**. For the Tetons, **driving is almost always the right call**.

## Drive (recommended)
- DEN → Jackson, WY: ~480 mi / **8–9 h** via I-80 W → US-191 N (Pinedale)
- Free, lets you bring all your gear, easy to extend trip
- Park at **Lupine Meadows trailhead** (free, fills early on weekends — arrive 5 a.m. or sleep in your car)

## Fly
- DEN → **JAC** (Jackson Hole) — multiple daily flights, ~1h 30m
- JAC is inside the park; **rental car required** to get to Lupine Meadows (~15 min)
- Alternative: DEN → **IDA** (Idaho Falls) — usually cheaper, ~2h drive to Jackson over Teton Pass (closed in winter / spring storms)

## Flight rules (if flying)
- **Carry on:** harness, helmet, headlamp, prescription meds, climbing shoes
- **Check:** rack (hardware confiscated at TSA), rope, pack, light puffy, helmet (if pack-checked)
- **Lithium batteries:** carry-on only
