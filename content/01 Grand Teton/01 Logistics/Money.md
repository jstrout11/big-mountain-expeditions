# Money — Grand Teton

Budget envelopes per person (USD):

| Tier | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Cheap | ~$400 |
| Realistic | ~$800 |
| Comfortable | ~$1,500 |

## Itemized — drive version (realistic)

| Item | USD |
|---|---:|
| Drive DEN-Jackson round-trip (fuel) | 200 |
| AAC Climbers Ranch, 3 nights | 135 |
| Backcountry permit (1 night Lower Saddle) | 50 |
| Park entrance (or America the Beautiful) | 35 / 80 |
| Food (3 days + climb day) | 80 |
| Misc / buffer | 100 |
| **Subtotal** | **~$600–800** |

## Itemized — fly version (add to above)

| Item | USD |
|---|---:|
| DEN-JAC round-trip flights | 250–400 |
| Rental car 3 days | 200 |
| **Add** | **+$450–600** |

## Cards & cash

- US domestic, any card works
- $40–80 cash for trailhead tips and beer money
- AAC card for ranch + emergency rescue benefit
- America the Beautiful pass if you have one (saves $35 park entrance)

## Tipping
- No guide on unguided trips, so no guide tip
- If you hire **a single Exum skills day** as a Mont Blanc warm-up, tip 10–15% of fee (~$60–90 cash)
