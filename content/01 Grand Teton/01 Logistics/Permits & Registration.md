# Permits & Registration — Grand Teton

## Backcountry camping permit (required for Lower Saddle)

- **Issuing agency:** Grand Teton National Park
- **Where:** [recreation.gov backcountry permits](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/4675317) — search "Grand Teton Backcountry"
- **Cost:** ~$45 reservation fee + $5/person/night
- **Lottery / early-bird:** opens **early January** for the summer season — applications taken in January, results in late January. For Lower Saddle in July/August, **enter the early-access window**.
- **Walk-up:** ~1/3 of permits held for walk-up — Jenny Lake Ranger Station, day before. Lower Saddle in peak season can be tight. Show up at 0700 the day before.
- **Climbing zone:** specify "Garnet Canyon — Lower Saddle"

## Climbing fee / registration

- **No separate climbing fee** — backcountry permit covers it
- **No mandatory registration** with rangers, but **Jenny Lake Rangers** maintain an informal climbers register at the [Jenny Lake Ranger Station](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/jennylake.htm). Stop by — they have current route conditions, snow on Owen-Spalding, what gear's needed.

## Park entrance

- **Grand Teton National Park entrance fee** — $35/vehicle, 7 days, or America the Beautiful pass ($80/year, covers all NPS sites)
