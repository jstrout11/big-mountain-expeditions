# Insurance — Grand Teton

US domestic trip in a national park. Wyoming SAR is taxpayer-funded for true emergencies, so the rescue side is largely free. The medical side is your standard US health insurance.

## Recommended stack

- **AAC (American Alpine Club) Rescue Benefit** — $95/year membership, includes up to **$15,000 SAR** + **$5,000 medical** worldwide. Pays for itself if you climb regularly and stay at the AAC Climbers Ranch (member discount).
- **Standard US health insurance** — confirm coverage at **St John's Health, Jackson, WY** (closest hospital; Level III trauma)
- **Travel insurance:** not really needed for a domestic Teton trip unless you want trip cancellation coverage

`teton_insurance:: AAC Rescue Benefit (+US health insurance)`
`primary_hospital:: St John's Health, Jackson WY`

## What rescue actually costs in Wyoming

- **Helicopter SAR (Teton County SAR + park rangers):** $0 if it's a SAR emergency (taxpayer-funded; Wyoming statute prohibits SAR billing)
- **Hospital ground ambulance** Jackson → St John's: $1,000–3,000 (covered by health insurance)
- **Medevac to bigger trauma center (Salt Lake or Denver):** $5,000–25,000 — health insurance + air ambulance plans cover most

## Things to verify on AAC Rescue Benefit

- [ ] **Activity coverage:** "rock climbing" and "mountaineering" both included
- [ ] **No altitude cap** at Teton elevations (well below any AAC RB threshold)
- [ ] **Membership active** — auto-renews if you set it that way

## What standard travel policies miss

Worth noting for future trips: standard travel policies often **exclude** technical climbing. For Teton this rarely matters (SAR is free, US health insurance covers the rest), but for Mont Blanc and Denali this is the crux.
