# Weather & Conditions — Grand Teton

## Climbing season

| Month | Climbable | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| May | No | Snow on route, avalanche danger |
| June | Partial | Snow still on Upper Saddle, ice in chimney |
| **July (mid → end)** | **Yes** | Snow patches melting out; some years still snow on upper route |
| **August** | **Yes** | Peak season. Most stable weather. Most crowded. |
| **September (early)** | **Yes — sweet spot** | Cooler, less crowded, weather often best |
| September (late) | Marginal | Early snow possible; check forecast |
| October+ | No | Winter conditions |

`teton_target_window:: late August to mid-September`

## Primary forecast sources

- **[Mountain Forecast — Grand Teton](https://www.mountain-forecast.com/peaks/Grand-Teton/forecasts/4197)**: elevation-resolved forecast at 4,200 m, 3,000 m, 1,500 m. **Best general purpose** for the Grand.
- **[NWS Riverton, WY area forecast](https://www.weather.gov/riw/)**: official US service, point forecasts you can pull for the Lower Saddle (43.7297, -110.8067).
- **[NOAA Spot Forecast tool](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=43.7405&lon=-110.8021)**: paste Lower Saddle coordinates, get text + graphical forecast.
- **[Open Snow / Joel Gratz Colorado-Wyoming](https://opensnow.com/dailysnow/colorado)**: human-curated, especially good for storm timing
- **[Jenny Lake Ranger Station](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/jennylake.htm)**: in person, day-of, **best for current route conditions** (snow on Upper Saddle, anchor condition at rappel)

## Climbing-weather priorities

1. **Lightning** — afternoon thunderstorms June–September. **Be off summit by noon.** Watch for morning CAPE buildup or convective signature.
2. **Wind on Lower Saddle** — sustained 30+ mph makes the bivy miserable and the Eye of the Needle scrambling more committing.
3. **Snow on the route** — early-season concern. Confirm in person with rangers + climbers descending.
4. **Temperature on summit ridge** — typically –5 to +5 °C summit ridge in August.

## Reading the Teton forecast

### Indicators of a good window
- 500mb height >588 dm over WY = stable ridge — best
- Low-relative-humidity profile mid-troposphere (drying) = less convection
- Surface dewpoints in Jackson <50°F (~10°C) = drier air, fewer thunderstorms
- 700mb winds < 30 kt = manageable upper-mountain wind

### Indicators of a bad window
- Cold front / mid-tropospheric trough moving through within 24 h
- Surface dewpoints 55°F+ + warm afternoon → afternoon storms likely
- 500mb winds 50+ kt over the Tetons → high-wind summit day
- Convective outlook (SPC) showing "Marginal Risk" or higher anywhere over Wyoming

## Practical decision framework

| Forecast feature | Action |
|---|---|
| Storms only **after** 14:00 | Go — earlier alpine start (03:00 from Saddle), off summit by 11:00 |
| Storms **starting** 11:00 or earlier | No-go for summit day; consider descent or rest day |
| 500mb winds 45+ kt at summit elevation | No-go (Lower Saddle + chimney exposure unsafe) |
| Snow forecast in 24 h | Drop down to Meadows or descend; refrigerate plans |
| Stable high pressure 3+ days | Ideal — schedule any technical day |
