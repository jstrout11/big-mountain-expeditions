# Training & Skills — Grand Teton

## Demand profile

- **Approach:** 6.5 mi with overnight pack, ~5,000 ft gain
- **Summit day:** alpine start, scramble + 2 short technical pitches, 9–12 hour day, ~3,000 ft gain
- **Altitude:** 4,199 m / 13,775 ft summit, one night at 3,540 m
- **Technical:** 5.4 trad climbing, simple multi-pitch, rappel descent

`teton_training_lead_time_weeks:: 8`

## Lead time

**8 weeks of focused training** on top of your existing fitness base is plenty. Most of the 50K ultra training already does the work.

## Specific training

- Long weekend hikes 12+ mi with 4,000+ ft gain wearing 30–40 lb pack
- Front Range 14ers (Quandary, Bierstadt, Grays-Torreys) for altitude exposure — at least one in the 2 weeks before
- Rock-climbing practice on 5.6–5.8 trad routes; lead a Grand-style chimney pitch a few times pre-trip
- One **multi-pitch trad day** with the rope partner you'll climb the OS with — make sure transitions are smooth

## Sample weekly structure (8 weeks out → trip)

| Day | Workout |
|---|---|
| Mon | Strength 1: heavy lifts (squat, deadlift, press, pull-ups) — 45 min |
| Tue | Run 60–90 min easy + mobility |
| Wed | Climbing gym 1.5 h — trad route practice |
| Thu | Run intervals or hills 45–60 min |
| Fri | Recovery / rest |
| Sat | **LONG DAY** — hike with pack 6–10 hr, 3,000–5,000 ft gain |
| Sun | Run easy 60 min OR rest |

## Technical skill checklist (drilled within 90 days of trip)

- [ ] **Trad lead at 5.4–5.6:** comfortable leading without hesitation
- [ ] **Anchor building:** 2-piece equalized anchor in <3 min with rack at hand
- [ ] **Multi-pitch transitions:** belay off anchor, swap leads, manage rope
- [ ] **Rappel + prussik backup:** smooth on single 70m
- [ ] **Knot review:** figure-8 follow-through, butterfly, prussik, clove, Munter

## Optional: Exum skills day

Consider a **single guided Exum or JHMG skills day** ($600 for 2:1) — anchor building, rappel, short-rope technique. Very valuable prep before Mont Blanc and beyond. See [[Guides & Operators]].

## Day-of fitness markers

If these are easy, you're ready:
- 4,000 ft gain with 30 lb pack in under 3 hours
- Lead 5.6 trad cleanly first try
- Sleep through a night at 12,000+ ft without major headache
