# Guides & Operators — Grand Teton

Vault default is **unguided** for the Owen-Spalding. Two Teton guide services are worth knowing about for skills days and partner options.

## Exum Mountain Guides

- **The** Teton guide service. Co-founded by Glenn Exum (Exum Ridge first ascent).
- Permanent base hut at Lower Saddle.
- **Programs:**
  - Guided Owen-Spalding 2-day: $1,200–1,500/person (2:1 typical)
  - Guided Exum Ridge: similar pricing
  - **Single-day skills clinics: $600/day for 2:1** — anchor building, rappel, short-rope technique. **Excellent prep value before Mont Blanc / Denali.**
  - 4-day technical climbing courses
- [exumguides.com](https://www.exumguides.com/)

## Jackson Hole Mountain Guides

- Second major outfit, equally reputable
- Often better availability than Exum on short notice
- Wider non-Grand programs (skiing, ice climbing, climbing schools)
- [jhmg.com](https://www.jhmg.com/)

## Decision framework

| Situation | Recommended approach |
|---|---|
| Doing OS unguided with experienced partner | **No guide needed** |
| Want a skills refresh pre-Mont Blanc | **Single Exum/JHMG skills day, ~$600 — best value of any guide service across the three trips** |
| Less-experienced partner | Hire Exum to guide them, you walk along (cheaper alternative to fully guiding both) |
| First-ever Grand, no partner | 2-day guided OS with Exum or JHMG |

## How to evaluate

- ✅ **IFMGA / AMGA certified guides** — both Exum and JHMG are
- ✅ **Liability insurance + permit compliance** — both fine
- ✅ **Recent successful trip reports / reviews** — Mountain Project, AAC forums
- ✅ **Conservative weather decision-making track record** — ask about turn-around stories
- ✅ **Clear gear / fitness expectations communicated in advance**

## Tipping conventions

| Service | Tip |
|---|---|
| Single-day skills clinic | 10–15% of fee (~$60–90 cash) |
| Multi-day guided OS | 5–10% of fee (~$100–150 cash) |
