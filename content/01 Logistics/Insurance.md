# Insurance

Standard travel insurance does **not** cover technical alpine climbing or helicopter rescue above ~4,500 m. You need a specialized policy. The single most important detail for all three trips is **"covers technical mountaineering with helicopter evacuation to a definitive medical facility."**

## What standard travel policies miss

- "Adventure sport" or "hazardous activity" exclusions that quietly void coverage above some elevation (often 4,000–6,000 m)
- No helicopter evac, or evac only "if medically necessary as determined by the insurer" (i.e., not when *you* need it)
- No coverage for crevasse rescue, fixed-line travel, technical rock/ice climbing
- $50k–$100k caps that are too low for an Anchorage-to-Seattle medevac (commonly $200k+)

## Recommended policies (climbing-specific)

### Global Rescue
- **What it is:** evacuation membership, not health insurance. Will field-extract you and transport to definitive care.
- **Cost:** ~$120 / week or ~$700 / year individual
- **Covers:** technical mountaineering to any elevation, with no per-incident cap on evacuation costs
- **Best for:** Denali especially. Most US guided operations require it or AAC Rescue.
- [globalrescue.com](https://www.globalrescue.com/)

### AAC (American Alpine Club) Rescue Benefit
- **What it is:** included with AAC membership ($95/year). Up to **$15,000 search-and-rescue** + **$5,000 medical expense** worldwide.
- **Cost:** $95/year — pays for itself if you climb regularly + use the AAC Climbers Ranch (member discount)
- **Limits:** the $15k cap won't cover an Alaska medevac alone, but it's a strong **secondary layer** on top of Global Rescue, and the membership is worth it for the Ranch and climbing community resources.
- [americanalpineclub.org](https://americanalpineclub.org/)

### IFMGA / Alpenverein cards
- **Austrian Alpenverein (ÖAV)** membership includes the **Alpenverein-Weltweit-Service** — worldwide alpine rescue + repatriation up to ~€25,000, **no altitude cap on standard alpine activities**
- **Cost:** ~€75/year
- **Best for:** any Alps trip. Many European refuges discount with ÖAV card.
- [alpenverein.at](https://www.alpenverein.at/)

### Standalone travel medical (for the medical side)
- **Garmin Response (formerly GEOS / inReach SAR)** — bundled with the inReach SOS subscription. Covers SAR costs only.
- **IMG Patriot Platinum / GeoBlue** — primary medical coverage abroad. Pair with Global Rescue for evac.

| Trip | Needs | Recommended stack |

| **Grand Teton OS** | US domestic, helicopter rescue is paid for by Wyoming if SAR; medical via standard US health insurance | AAC Rescue Benefit ($95/yr) as belt-and-suspenders. Confirm US health plan covers JH Memorial. |
| **Mont Blanc Trois Monts** | French helicopter rescue is billed to non-EU climbers; €5–15k typical | **ÖAV Alpenverein** ($75) covers it; OR **Global Rescue** + travel medical |

`teton_insurance:: AAC Rescue Benefit (+US health insurance)`
`mont_blanc_insurance:: ÖAV Alpenverein or Global Rescue`
`denali_insurance:: Global Rescue + AAC + primary US health plan`

## Things to verify on any policy

- [ ] **Activity coverage:** "technical mountaineering" or "alpinism" explicitly named — not just "trekking"
- [ ] **Altitude cap:** above your objective's summit (Denali: 20,310 ft / 6,191 m; many policies cap at 6,000 m)
- [ ] **Helicopter evac:** explicitly covered, no "medical necessity" gatekeeper clause
- [ ] **Repatriation:** transport home from foreign country covered (Mont Blanc)
- [ ] **Pre-existing conditions:** standard disclosure questions; review honestly
- [ ] **Solo / partner-roped:** some policies exclude solo climbing — Denali especially

## What rescue actually costs (ballpark, 2024–2025)

| Region | Helicopter pickup | Hospital transport | Medevac to home |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| US (Wyoming) | $0 (taxpayer-funded SAR) | $5–15k | covered by health insurance |
| France (Chamonix) | $5–15k for foreigners | $2–5k | $30–60k transatlantic |
| Alaska Range | $0 NPS for SAR; **$10–40k** for non-SAR helicopter | $5–20k | **$100k+** medevac to Lower 48 |

> [!warning] The non-SAR Denali helicopter case
> The NPS will SAR you for free **if it's a true emergency**. But moves like "evacuated due to HAPE but ambulatory" can fall outside the SAR mandate — that's when private medevac costs hit. This is the scenario Global Rescue exists for.
