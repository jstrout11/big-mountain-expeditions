# Permits & Registration

Each objective has a different permitting regime. Two of three need advance applications; one is essentially walk-up. Denali in particular needs a full 60-day lead time.

## Grand Teton — Owen-Spalding

### Backcountry camping permit (required for Lower Saddle)
- **Issuing agency:** Grand Teton National Park
- **Where:** [recreation.gov backcountry permits](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/4675317) — search "Grand Teton Backcountry"
- **Cost:** ~$45 reservation fee + $5/person/night
- **Lottery / early-bird:** opens **early January** for the summer season — applications taken in January, results in late January. For Lower Saddle in July/August, **you need to enter the early-access window**.
- **Walk-up:** ~1/3 of permits are held for walk-up — Jenny Lake Ranger Station, day before. Lower Saddle in peak season can be tight. Show up at 0700 the day before.
- **Climbing zone:** specify "Garnet Canyon — Lower Saddle"

### Climbing fee / registration
- **No separate climbing fee** for Grand Teton — backcountry permit covers it
- **No mandatory registration** with rangers, but **Jenny Lake Rangers** maintain an informal climbers register at the [Jenny Lake Ranger Station](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/jennylake.htm). Stop by — they have current route conditions, snow on Owen-Spalding, what gear's needed.

### Park entrance
- **Grand Teton National Park entrance fee** — $35/vehicle, 7 days, or America the Beautiful pass ($80/year, covers all NPS sites)

`teton_lead_time_days:: 30`

## Mont Blanc — Trois Monts

### No permit required to climb
- France imposes **no climbing permit** on Mont Blanc itself (this is a recent and ongoing debate — local Saint-Gervais mayor has periodically tried to require one for the **Goûter route** in summer; **Trois Monts is not affected** by these proposals)
- The mountain is open. You can walk up tomorrow if you have the skills and the weather.

### What you do need to book
- **Refuge du Cosmiques** — see [[Accommodations]]. Booking opens ~6 months out, fills fast for Jul–Aug weekends.
- **Aiguille du Midi téléphérique** — can be reserved online to avoid 1–2h summer queue. [Compagnie du Mont Blanc](https://www.montblancnaturalresort.com/)
- **PGHM Chamonix** — the Peloton de Gendarmerie de Haute Montagne is the mountain rescue service. **No registration required**, but it's strongly recommended to:
  - Leave a route plan at your refuge or with your accommodation
  - Carry the [SafeMountain Chamonix](https://chamonix.safemountain.fr/en) app — has route conditions and emergency reporting
  - Note the **PGHM Chamonix phone: +33 4 50 53 16 89**

### Health card / insurance card
- US travelers should carry their **travel insurance card with helicopter evac coverage** — French helicopter rescue is free *for residents/EU* but **billable to foreigners**. Standard rescue $5,000–15,000.
- See [[Insurance]] for which policies cover this.

`mont_blanc_lead_time_days:: 180`

## Denali — West Buttress

### The big one. Plan accordingly.

### NPS Denali climbing registration
- **Required for every climber** on Denali above 7,000 ft (i.e., everyone on West Buttress)
- **Lead time: 60 days minimum.** NPS requires the full registration packet **60 days before your climbing start date**. Late applications are not accepted.
- **Cost:**
  - **$425/person climbing fee** (2025 rate — check [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/mountaineering.htm) for current)
  - **Plus** $15 NPS entrance fee per climber
- **Register at:** [pay.gov Denali](https://www.pay.gov/) (NPS sends you the link after acceptance) or via the [Walter Harper Talkeetna Ranger Station](https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/mountaineering.htm)
- **What's in the packet:**
  - Personal climbing résumé (NPS wants to see relevant experience — Rainier, Cotopaxi, Chimborazo, plus glacier travel/crevasse rescue training will satisfy this)
  - Team roster — solo registrations allowed but heavily discouraged
  - Itinerary with start/end dates
  - Self-registration form

### In-person check-in at Talkeetna Ranger Station
- **Required:** the day before your fly-in. The station has reduced hours; check schedule.
- **Watch the orientation video** (~1 h, NPS-produced, mandatory)
- **Receive your CMC** (Clean Mountain Can — required human waste container for the upper mountain) and trash bags
- **Pre-trip inspection:** rangers will visually check that you have key gear (rope, axes, screws, anchors, fuel). They won't bag-check every item but they will ask.
- **File your itinerary** with the rangers. They check you back in on descent.

### On the mountain
- **NPS climbing rangers** patrol the route. Stop at the **14,200 Camp ranger camp** to check in if rangers are there. They are a serious safety asset; use them.

### Other notes
- **No special visa** for US citizens (it's the US). EU/foreign teams: standard ESTA / visa requirements.
- **Solo climbing:** legal but NPS will require you to demonstrate substantial relevant experience. Solo on Denali is technically allowed; partner-roped is the norm.
- **Fuel and stove inspection:** rangers will check that your stove is glove-friendly and your fuel containers are properly labeled.

`denali_lead_time_days:: 90`
