## Denali guided
- **AAI / Alpine Ascents / RMI / Mountain Trip:** $11,500–14,500/person for the standard 21-day West Buttress program
- **What's included:** guides, group gear (tents, ropes, fuel, group meals), permit fees, transportation Anchorage-Kahiltna round trip
- **What's NOT included:** personal gear, flights to Anchorage, lodging Anchorage / Talkeetna, tips ($800–1,500 typical)
- See [[Guides & Operators]] for evaluation

## Cards & cash

### Grand Teton
- US domestic — any card works. Bring a debit card for cash at trailheads (very rare you need it). Tip cash for guide if hired.

### Mont Blanc / Chamonix
- **Card-first:** France is heavily cards. Visa/MC accepted nearly everywhere including refuges.
- **Get a card with no foreign-transaction fee** (Chase Sapphire, Capital One Venture, Schwab debit) — typical 3% FTF adds up
- **Refuge wallet:** ~€50 small notes for shared refuge purchases (extra meal, beer)
- **PIN-required:** some unattended self-service kiosks require chip+PIN — confirm your card has a PIN before traveling

### Denali
- US domestic — easy. But once you're on the glacier, **money is meaningless**. Pre-pay everything.
- Bring cash for air taxi tips ($50–100 per pilot is standard if you had a hassle-free flight in and out)
- Bring small bills for Talkeetna — Roadhouse, gear last-minute, etc.

## Bank-of-the-mountain rule
Bring **one credit card + one debit card + cash equivalent of $200–500** depending on trip. Keep one card in a separate location from the other. Never put both in the same pocket.
