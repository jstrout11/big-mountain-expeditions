# Travel & Flights

Home airport is **DEN**. The three objectives have very different travel profiles.

## Grand Teton — Owen-Spalding

### Drive (recommended for most trips)
- DEN → Jackson, WY: ~480 mi / **8–9 h** via I-80 W → US-191 N (Pinedale)
- Free, lets you bring all your gear, easy to extend trip
- Park at **Lupine Meadows trailhead** (free, fills early on weekends — arrive 5 a.m. or sleep in your car)

### Fly
- DEN → **JAC** (Jackson Hole) — multiple daily flights, ~1h 30m
- JAC is in the park; **rental car required** to get to Lupine Meadows (~15 min)
- Alternative: DEN → **IDA** (Idaho Falls) — usually cheaper, ~2h drive to Jackson over Teton Pass (closed in winter / spring storms)

`teton_travel_default:: drive`

## Mont Blanc — Trois Monts

### Recommended routing
- **DEN → GVA (Geneva)** is the headline routing — closest major airport to Chamonix
  - United DEN→ZRH→GVA, Air France DEN→CDG→GVA, Lufthansa DEN→FRA→GVA, KLM DEN→AMS→GVA
  - Round trip ~$900–1500 economy depending on season; July–Aug is peak
- **GVA → Chamonix:** ~88 km, ~1h 15m
  - Shared shuttle: [Mountain Drop-offs](https://www.mountaindropoffs.com/), [AlpyBus](https://www.alpybus.com/), [SAT Chamonix](https://www.sat-montblanc.com/) — ~€35–50 each way, must pre-book
  - Private taxi: €200–250 each way
  - Train: GVA → St-Gervais → Chamonix on Mont Blanc Express, ~3h with transfer, ~€30

### Alternatives
- **DEN → ZRH (Zurich)** — sometimes cheaper, drive 4h to Chamonix or train 6h with transfers
- **DEN → CDG (Paris)** — long train to Chamonix (~6h via Lyon), rarely better unless paired with Paris stop
- **DEN → LYS (Lyon)** — closer than CDG but fewer transatlantic options

> [!tip] Carry-on the boots
> Always carry on double mountaineering boots and harness/helmet. Checked-bag mishandling can end the trip. Pack a small duffel inside checked luggage for the climb (lockers at Chamonix accommodation handle the rest).

`mont_blanc_travel_default:: DEN-GVA via European hub`

## Denali — West Buttress

This is the only one of the three that involves a small-plane glacier landing. Plan the **transfer day** as carefully as the climbing.

### Stage 1 — Lower 48 to Anchorage
- **DEN → ANC** — usually 1 stop (SEA most common)
- Alaska Airlines, Delta, United all serve ANC
- Round-trip ~$500–800 economy outside peak summer
- Plan to overnight ANC outbound (get groceries, fuel canisters, last-minute gear) and likely overnight on return

### Stage 2 — Anchorage to Talkeetna
- **Drive:** ~115 mi / **2h 30m** north on AK-3. Rent a car at ANC. Most teams do this.
- **Bus:** [Park Connection Motorcoach](https://www.alaskacoach.com/) ANC → Talkeetna, ~3h, ~$80
- **Train:** Alaska Railroad Denali Star, scenic but only runs **once daily** in summer and pricier
- Stay in Talkeetna 1 night minimum — register with the [Walter Harper Talkeetna Ranger Station](https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/mountaineering.htm) the day before flying in

### Stage 3 — Talkeetna to Kahiltna base camp (glacier flight)
- **Air taxi operators (pick one and book early):**
  - [Talkeetna Air Taxi (TAT)](https://www.talkeetnaair.com/) — Otters and Beavers
  - [K2 Aviation](https://flyk2.com/)
  - [Sheldon Air Service](https://sheldonairservice.com/)
- **Cost:** ~$700–900/person round trip with climbing gear allowance
- **Weather delays are routine.** Plan **2–4 buffer days** on each end. Air taxi will sit you on the ground until the Kahiltna landing strip is flyable.
- **Hauling allowance:** typically ~125 lb total (you + gear). Anything over is excess weight $.
- Flights land on the Southeast Fork of Kahiltna Glacier at **7,200 ft (2,200 m)** — that's where you start.

### Stage 4 — Return
- Same air taxi flies you out. Same weather caveat — could be hours, could be days.
- ANC → DEN return: **build in 2 buffer nights**. Many teams sit in Talkeetna 1–3 extra days fly-out delayed.

`denali_travel_buffer_days:: 4`

## Cross-cutting flight rules

- **Always carry on:** boots, harness, helmet, prescription meds, key insulation layer, headlamp
- **Check:** pack, sleeping bag, stoves (empty, never fueled), tents, hardware (in checked bag, never carry-on — pickets and axes will be confiscated)
- **Fuel:** never fly with fuel canisters or white gas. Buy on arrival. Talkeetna AMH and Ascending Path Outfitters stock IsoPro / white gas.
- **Lithium batteries:** loose batteries carry-on only (no quantity limit for personal devices under 100 Wh each, but tape terminals)
