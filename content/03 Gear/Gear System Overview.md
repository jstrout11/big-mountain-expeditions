# Gear System Overview

Three trips, three quite different gear loadouts. Lots of overlap on personal items (harness, helmet, layers) but big divergence on footwear, sleep system, and team gear.

## What's shared across all three

- **Harness:** light alpine (Petzl Sitta, BD Couloir)
- **Helmet:** lightweight climbing helmet (Petzl Sirocco / Meteor)
- **Belay device:** ATC Guide or Reverso
- **Headlamp:** Petzl Actik Core + spare batteries / Nao
- **Sunglasses:** **Cat 4 glacier glasses with side shields** (Julbo Vermont, Spektrum Östra) for Mont Blanc + Denali; Cat 3 OK for Teton
- **Sunscreen:** mineral, SPF 50+, **on stick** form for face and ears
- **Lip balm:** SPF 30+
- **First aid kit:** scaled to trip — see per-trip lists
- **Watch with altimeter / barometer:** Suunto, Garmin Fenix
- **Phone in waterproof case**
- **Camera:** if bringing — see [[#Photography weight tradeoffs]]
- **Trekking poles:** light Z-poles (Black Diamond Distance Carbon) — useful on all three

## What diverges

| System | Teton | Mont Blanc | Denali |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Boots** | Approach shoes (La Sportiva TX4) | Single-skin mountaineering (La Sportiva Nepal Cube, Scarpa Mont Blanc Pro) or 4000m double | **8000 m double** (Olympus Mons Cube, Phantom 8000) |
| **Crampons** | Light steel (BD Sabretooth) if any | 12-pt steel anti-balling (Petzl Vasak / Lynx) | 12-pt steel (Petzl Lynx with vertical-front for Denali Pass) |
| **Axe** | Optional, 50 cm walking | 50–55 cm walking | **55–60 cm walking + 1 technical for fixed lines** |
| **Sleeping bag** | n/a or 20 °F | 0–20 °F (refuge has bedding) | **–20 to –40 °F down** |
| **Pad** | n/a | inflatable, R 3+ | **two pads: foam + inflatable, R 5+ combined** |
| **Tent** | n/a (hut/bivy at Saddle) | n/a (refuge) | **4-season expedition** (Hilleberg Keron 3, BD First Light + vestibule) |
| **Stove** | n/a or single canister | n/a (refuge) | **Two white-gas stoves** (MSR XGK EX × 2) |
| **Pack** | 35–40 L | 35–45 L summit, 50 L approach | **75–90 L plus sled** |
| **Insulated parka** | Light puffy | Mid-weight down or synthetic (Patagonia DAS Light) | **Heavyweight down** (FF Volant, Rab Expedition) |
| **Mittens** | n/a | Light insulated gloves | **Down mittens + liner gloves + spare**: 3-glove system |
| **Sled** | n/a | n/a | **Yes — paris/cmu sled + harness** |
| **Skis or snowshoes** | n/a | n/a | **MSR Lightning Ascent + skis if you ski (faster)** |

## Layering principles (all trips)

1. **Base layer:** synthetic or merino, never cotton. Long-sleeve crew or 1/4 zip.
2. **Active mid:** light fleece (R1) or synthetic active insulation (Patagonia Nano-Air Light)
3. **Soft shell:** wind-resistant, breathable — most-worn layer on the mountain
4. **Hard shell:** Gore-Tex Pro or eVent jacket + pants, ALL pit/leg zips
5. **Belay puffy:** mid-weight (Mont Blanc) or heavyweight (Denali) for stops + emergencies
6. **Down suit:** Denali only, optional for stronger climbers, mandatory for first-timers

## Photography weight tradeoffs

| Setup | Weight | Use case |
|---|---:|---|
| Phone in chest pocket | ~250 g | Default for all 3 trips |
| Sony RX100 / Ricoh GR III | ~500 g | Quality jump, no lens swap, Denali viable |
| Mirrorless + 1 lens (Sony A7C / Fuji X-T) | ~1100 g | Mont Blanc OK, Denali = serious cost |
| DSLR + multiple lenses | 3 kg+ | Don't |

**Recommendation:** phone for Teton + Denali, Ricoh GR III for Mont Blanc (lower weight cost, big quality gain).

## Where to buy / rent

### Buy
- **[Backcountry](https://www.backcountry.com/)** — fast US shipping, good return policy
- **[REI](https://www.rei.com/)** — boots in person, member dividend
- **[Feathered Friends Seattle](https://featheredfriends.com/)** — best down parkas and bags in the US
- **[Outdoor Gear Exchange](https://www.gearx.com/)** — used and discount
- **[CampSaver](https://www.campsaver.com/)** — good prices
- **[Hyperlite Mountain Gear](https://www.hyperlitemountaingear.com/)** — UL packs and tarps
- **[Patagonia outlet](https://wornwear.patagonia.com/)** — used gear at real discount

### Rent (Denali especially)
- **[Alaska Mountaineering & Hiking (AMH)](https://www.alaskamountaineering.com/)** — Anchorage, rents 8000 m boots, sleeping bags, sleds. Solid choice if you only do Denali once.
- **[Mountain Hardware Anchorage](https://www.mountainhardware.com/anchorage)** — alt rental shop
- **[Mountain Madness](https://www.mountainmadness.com/)** — rents items as part of guided programs
