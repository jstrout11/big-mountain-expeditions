# Technical Skills

The minimum technical skill stack for unguided attempts on each route. Skills atrophy fast — drill them within 90 days of each trip.

## Per-trip skill requirements

| Skill | Teton OS | Mont Blanc | Denali |

## Critical skill descriptions

### Glacier travel — rope-team protocol
- **Rope spacing:** 10–15 m between climbers on rope team of 2, 8–10 m on team of 3
- **Coil + butterfly** the remaining rope on shoulders for hauling pulling
- **Tension management:** rope between climbers slightly off the snow (taut enough to feel a fall)
- **Knot system:** butterfly knots every 3–4 m for friction in a crevasse fall — debated; Alaska Range parties often forgo for sled-team flexibility
- **Pace:** slow and steady. Don't bunch up.

### Crevasse rescue — 3:1 Z-pulley
The core unguided skill for Mont Blanc and Denali. Must be able to set this up from arrest in **under 5 minutes**.

1. Self-arrest, dig in hard, build initial anchor (picket or buried axe)
2. Transfer load to anchor with prussik on rope
3. Take in slack rope from victim side
4. Set up 3:1 mechanical advantage:
   - Anchor → climber rope
   - Prussik on victim side rope → carabiner → pulley/biner back to anchor
   - Pull free strand to haul
5. Reset prussik when it reaches the redirect carabiner; haul again
6. Communicate with victim — if they can ascend on their own, much faster

**Drill:** simulate fall with 80 kg of pack/sled hanging off carabiner over a ledge or strong tree branch. Practice until reflexive.

### Self-arrest
- **Feet up, axe pick into the slope**, body across slope, head pointed up
- 4 attitudes to practice: head-up forward, head-down forward (most dangerous), head-up backward, head-down backward
- **Practice on 25–35° slope** with safe runout. Spring snow on Colorado 14ers ideal.

### Steep snow front-pointing (Mont Blanc Maudit slope, Denali fixed lines)
- **Crampons:** plant front points firmly, weight directly over them
- **Axe:** dagger position (low slopes), self-belay above shoulder (mid slopes), pied a plat (low-angle ice)
- **Pace:** kick-step rhythm, 3 points of contact ideally
- **Practice:** any steep snow couloir on a Colorado 14er in spring (Holy Cross, Mountainview), front-pointing up + down

### Fixed-line ascending (Denali)
- **Ascender:** Petzl Ascension, right-hand version (mirror for left-handed) — clipped to your harness with a short sling
- **Backup prussik or second ascender** below if rope is shared between teams
- **Smooth transitions** at intermediate anchors: arrest top ascender, transfer below, release top
- **Practice:** any fixed-rope wall (climbing gym, indoor wall) or rig a line from a tree

### Snow anchors
- **Picket (24"–30"):** vertical placement in firm snow, mid-shaft sling at 60° angle for max strength; horizontal "deadman" placement for softer snow
- **T-slot:** dig 60-cm-deep T-shaped slot, lay picket horizontally, bury, sling from bottom of T
- **Buried axe:** axe horizontal in slot, sling around shaft midpoint
- **Bollard:** carved snow column, sling around — useful for rappels

### Camp building (Denali-specific)
- **Snow walls:** quarry blocks 50 × 30 × 20 cm with snow saw, stack to wind-block
- **Tent platform:** stomp out flat area 3 × 3 m, let cure 30 min before pitching
- **Anchor system:** every guyline + main stake = picket, T-slot, or carabiner-clipped axe
- **Vestibule for cooking + storage**
- **Pee hole** uphill from tent, downwind
- **CMC location** downwind, marked, 30 m from tent

## Where to learn / refresh

### Courses (1–5 days)
- **Colorado Mountain School** — multi-day glacier travel + crevasse rescue
- **American Alpine Institute** — Cascades-based, full alpine programs
- **Petzl Foundation training videos** — free online (https://www.petzl.com/INT/en/Sport/Skills)
- **AAC chapters** — local member education days

### Self-led practice locations (Colorado)
- **St Mary's Glacier (45 min from Denver)** — small but real glacier surface for drills
- **Front Range 14ers in spring** (Quandary, Holy Cross, Castle) — steep snow practice
- **Local climbing gym** — anchor building, multi-pitch transitions, rappel practice
- **Backyard / garage** — fixed-rope ascension setup, prussik knot practice

## Skill freshness audit checklist (pre-trip, 90-day version)

- [ ] **Crevasse rescue 3:1**: set up from arrest in <5 min, drilled with rope partner
- [ ] **Self-arrest**: drilled in 4 positions on real snow within 30 days
- [ ] **Anchor building**: 2-piece equalized anchor in <3 min with rack at hand
- [ ] **Rappel + prussik backup**: smooth on single + double rope
- [ ] **Fixed-line ascending**: smooth transitions at intermediate anchors
- [ ] **Snow anchor**: built picket and deadman in target conditions
- [ ] **Knot review**: figure-8 follow-through, butterfly, prussik, Bachmann, clove, Munter
