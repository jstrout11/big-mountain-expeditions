# Training Plan

Each objective demands a different mix of endurance, strength, and technical skill. The good news: ultra training overlaps significantly with all three. Layer specific work on top.

## Phase model

The recommended structure across all three goals:

| Phase | Length | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Base** | 4–8 mo out | Aerobic base, strength foundation |
| **Build** | 2–4 mo out | Mountain-specific endurance, skills |
| **Peak** | 4–6 wk out | Trip-specific volume, taper |
| **Taper** | 1–2 wk out | Drop volume, maintain intensity |

## Per-expedition demands

### Grand Teton OS — short and sharp
- **Demand:** 12 mi round trip with 7,000 ft gain over 2 days. Climbing on top of fatigue. Altitude 13,775 ft.
- **Specific training:**
  - Long weekend hikes 12+ mi with 4,000+ ft gain wearing 30–40 lb pack
  - Front Range 14ers (Quandary, Bierstadt, Grays-Torreys) for altitude exposure
  - Rock-climbing practice on 5.6–5.8 trad routes; lead a Grand-style chimney pitch a few times pre-trip
  - **Lead time:** 8 weeks of focused training over your existing fitness base is plenty
- **Carryover from 50K training:** ~all of it

### Mont Blanc Trois Monts — alpine endurance + glacier skills
- **Demand:** 10–14 hr summit day, ~4,000 ft gain on summit push, at 4,800 m, technical glacier + steep snow
- **Specific training:**
  - Long hikes 6–10 hr with pack, simulating alpine start (3 a.m. wake)
  - Glacier skills + crevasse rescue refresh (drill 2× in the 3 months before)
  - Front Range 14ers — at least 3 in the month before, with one at 14k+ overnight if possible
  - Front-pointing practice on steep snow (any Colorado 14er couloir in spring: Holy Cross, Quandary, Castle)
  - 1–2 rock-climbing days a week at the gym to maintain hand strength + harness familiarity
  - **Lead time:** 4–6 months of focused training. Ideally do Grand Teton ~6 weeks before as a tune-up.
- **Carryover from 50K training:** strong. The 10-hour summit day is similar physiology.

### Denali West Buttress — multi-day load haul + altitude + cold
- **Demand:** 21 days on glacier, hauling 60–80 lb sled + 60–70 lb pack, summit at 6,191 m, –40 °F potential
- **Specific training:** this is the real plan, takes ~6–12 months
  - **Endurance:** maintain 50K-trained aerobic base year-round
  - **Strength:** twice weekly. Squats, deadlifts, step-ups with weight, single-leg work. Sled drag if you can find empty parking lots / sand.
  - **Pack carries:** weekly long hikes with 50–70 lb pack, 6–10 hours, 3,000–5,000 ft gain. Build to **70 lb / 8 hours / 4,000 ft** comfortably.
  - **Sled drag simulation:** tire drag on dirt roads; loaded jogger stroller on packed snow; weighted backpack as proxy. Hard-find but valuable.
  - **Altitude:** repeated 14er exposure all summer/fall before trip; consider altitude tent (Hypoxico) 4–6 weeks pre-trip for sleep adaptation
  - **Cold exposure:** practice sleeping out at –20 °F at least 3 nights; test full layering system
  - **Skills:** crevasse rescue, fixed-line ascending, snow anchor practice, glacier travel
  - **Lead time:** **12 months ideal**, 6 months minimum after a solid base.
- **Carryover from 50K training:** strong base. **But ultra-running is volume-focused; Denali demands load-carry strength specifically.** Add the load work.

## Sample weekly structure (Build phase, all trips)

| Day | Workout |
|---|---|
| Mon | Strength 1: heavy lifts (squat, deadlift, press, pull-ups) — 45 min |
| Tue | Run 60–90 min easy + mobility |
| Wed | Strength 2: single-leg, core, stabilizer — 45 min |
| Thu | Run intervals or hills 45–60 min |
| Fri | Recovery / rest |
| Sat | **LONG DAY** — hike with pack 6–10 hr, 3,000–5,000 ft gain |
| Sun | Run easy 60 min OR rest |

For Denali specifically, do **back-to-back long pack days** (Sat + Sun) once a month in build phase to simulate consecutive carry days.

## Strength essentials

- **Back squat** — work to 1.5× body weight for 5 reps
- **Deadlift** — work to 1.75× body weight for 5 reps
- **Bulgarian split squat** — 30 lb in each hand for 8 reps each leg
- **Step-ups** — 24" box, 30 lb each hand, 8 reps each leg
- **Pull-ups** — 10 strict for body weight
- **Single-leg deadlift** — 50% of regular deadlift for 8 reps each leg
- **Loaded carry** (farmer's, sled drag) — 30+ min weekly

## Cardio benchmarks (for Denali)

- **Vertical kilometer test:** climb 1,000 m of elevation gain, fastest you can. Sub-1:00 strong, sub-1:15 reasonable.
- **Loaded vertical:** 1,000 m gain with 50 lb pack — target sub-2:00
- **Heart-rate-controlled climb:** climb 3,000 ft with HR capped at 75% max — should feel sustainable for 6+ hours

## Specific drill sessions

### Crevasse rescue / glacier travel
- Practice setting **3:1 z-pulley** with progress capture from harness in under 5 min
- Practice **prussik ascent** of rope (15 m): under 2 min from hanging
- **Self-arrest** drills on 30° snow: forward, backward, head-first
- **Snow anchor** practice: bury picket, set deadman, build T-slot
- Drill 2–3 times in the 90 days before any glacier trip (esp. before Mont Blanc and Denali)

### Fixed-line work (Denali-specific)
- **Petzl Ascension** smooth on/off transitions
- **Body position** on steep ground: feet planted, ascender below shoulder
- **Rappel transitions** at intermediate anchors
- Find a steep practice fixed line on a cliff or via ferrata-style setup

### Rock skills (Teton-specific)
- Trad lead at 5.4–5.8 grade comfortably
- **Anchor building:** equalized 2-piece + cordelette anchor in under 5 min
- **Multi-pitch transitions:** belay off anchor, swap leads
- **Rappel:** single-rope + double-rope setup, with backup prussik

## Lead-time calendar (12-month Denali example)

| Month | Phase | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| -12 | Off-season | Maintain base, recover, ski |
| -10 | Base | Build aerobic mileage, gym strength 3x/wk |
| -8 | Base | Add long hikes 4–5 hr, climb a Front Range 14er |
| -6 | Base→Build | Start loaded pack hikes, increase strength |
| -4 | Build | Heavy load weekend days; book gear; submit NPS reg |
| -3 | Build | Back-to-back load weekends; cold sleep test |
| -2 | Build→Peak | Glacier skills weekend; final big training block |
| -1 | Peak→Taper | Final pack-carry weekend; pre-trip 14er; taper |
| 0 | Trip | |
