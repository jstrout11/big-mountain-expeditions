# Resources

## Books

### Skills + training
- **Steve House & Scott Johnston — *Training for the New Alpinism* (2014)** — *the* current standard reference. Build periodization, mountain-specific endurance, strength integration. The framework everything in [[Training Plan]] is built on.
- **Steve House & Scott Johnston — *Uphill Athlete* (2019)** — follow-up, broader application
- **Mark Houston & Kathy Cosley — *Alpine Climbing: Techniques to Take You Higher*** — practical alpine skill manual
- **John Long & Bob Gaines — *Climbing Anchors*** — anchor building deep dive

### Route-specific
- **Colby Coombs — *Denali's West Buttress: A Climber's Guide to Mt. McKinley's Classic Route*** — the standard Denali WB reference. Essential.
- **François Damilano — *Le Mont Blanc* (French)** — most detailed Mont Blanc routes book
- **Renny Jackson & Leigh Ortenburger — *A Climber's Guide to the Teton Range*** — the bible for Teton climbing
- **Joe Stock — *The Alaska Factor: Notes from a Life in Alpine Climbing*** — context for Alaska Range climbing

### Health + altitude
- **Andrew Pollard, David Murdoch — *The High Altitude Medicine Handbook*** — clinical-grade reference
- **Stephen Bezruchka — *Altitude Illness: Prevention & Treatment*** — concise practical guide

### Big-mountain perspective + mindset
- **Anatoli Boukreev & G. Weston DeWalt — *The Climb*** — the contrarian Everest 1996 account
- **Ed Viesturs & David Roberts — *No Shortcuts to the Top*** — the 14 8000ers without supplemental O₂
- **Steve House — *Beyond the Mountain*** — modern alpinism philosophy
- **Bernadette McDonald — *Tomaž Humar*** — biography, hard alpinism perspective

## Films / video

- **NOVA: Surviving Denali (2014)** — solid intro
- **Sherpa (2015)** — Everest commercial-climbing context, transferable lessons
- **The Alpinist (2021, Marc-André Leclerc)** — modern solo alpinism portrait
- **Free Solo (2018)** — technical but more rock-relevant
- **Petzl Foundation skills videos** — [petzl.com skills library](https://www.petzl.com/INT/en/Sport/Skills)
- **AMGA YouTube channel** — short skill tutorials

## Websites + communities

### General reference
- **[Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/)** — route topos, recent ticks, comments
- **[SummitPost](https://www.summitpost.org/)** — older but extensive route descriptions
- **[Camptocamp.org](https://www.camptocamp.org/)** — European routes, especially good for Alps
- **[American Alpine Club resources](https://americanalpineclub.org/library)** — accident reports, climbing journals
- **[Accidents in North American Climbing (AAC annual)](https://publications.americanalpineclub.org/articles/13201213900)** — annual lessons-learned

### Training
- **[Uphill Athlete](https://www.uphillathlete.com/)** — articles, training plans, paid coaching
- **[Training Beta](https://www.trainingbeta.com/)** — climbing-specific strength training
- **[Mountain Tactical Institute](https://mtntactical.com/)** — load-carry and tactical fitness, useful for Denali

### Weather
- **[Mountain Forecast](https://www.mountain-forecast.com/)** — elevation-resolved forecasts
- **[Météo France mountain bulletins](https://meteofrance.com/meteo-montagne)** — France's official source
- **[NWS Anchorage](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=63.0692&lon=-151.0070)** — Denali forecasts
- **[Weatherspark](https://weatherspark.com/)** — climatology reference
- **[OpenSnow](https://opensnow.com/)** — snow-focused forecasting (Teton-relevant)

### Conditions
- **[La Chamoniarde](https://www.chamoniarde.com/)** — Chamonix route conditions
- **[Office de Haute Montagne](https://www.ohm-chamonix.com/)** — Chamonix mountain info
- **[NPS Denali Mountaineering Bulletins](https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/mountaineering.htm)** — official Denali updates
- **[Mountain Project route ticks](https://www.mountainproject.com/)** — recent climber notes
