# Gear List — Denali

The serious one. Total weight (pack + sled) **120–140 lb / 55–63 kg per person** for a 21-day unguided trip. Pack: 60–70 lb. Sled: 50–80 lb.

## Footwear — 8000 m double boots only

- **La Sportiva Olympus Mons Cube** ($1,000) — current state-of-the-art light expedition boot
- **Scarpa Phantom 8000** ($1,000) — direct competitor
- Older option: **Millet Everest** — heavier but reliable
- **DO NOT** climb Denali in single-skin boots. Frostbite injury is near-certain.
- **Vapor barrier socks** (RBH Designs Vaprtherm or DIY plastic bag) for high camp + summit day — keeps boot insulation dry over multi-day cold
- 4 pairs heavy wool ski socks + 2 pair liners

## Camp shoes

- Insulated booties / overboots for camp (Feathered Friends Down Booties or similar)
- Used inside tent vestibule + short trips to "outhouse"

## Outerwear — Denali layering

| Layer | Item | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Base 1 | Wool/synth long-sleeve crew | 200 g |
| Base 2 | Heavyweight thermal top (Capilene Air Crew) | 300 g |
| Active mid | R1 hoodie or Patagonia Nano-Air Hoody | 400 g |
| Soft shell | Wind-resistant soft shell (Mammut Aenergy, BD Alpine) | 500 g |
| Hard shell jacket | Gore-Tex Pro shell (Arc'teryx Alpha SV, Patagonia Triolet) | 500 g |
| Belay puffy | **Heavyweight down parka** (FF Volant, Rab Expedition, Arc'teryx Cerium SV) — fits over hard shell | 1,000 g |
| Down suit (optional) | One-piece down suit for high camp + summit (FF Frontpoint, RAB Expedition Suit) | 1,800 g |

A **down suit** vs **parka + bibs** is a real choice:
- **Suit:** less complicated, no waist gap, best below –40 °F. Awkward on/off; not breathable.
- **Parka + insulated bibs:** more versatile, layers better at 14k Camp where you'll sit for days. **Recommend for first Denali.**

### Pants layering
- Base layer pants (wool/synthetic, heavyweight)
- Soft shell pants (Mammut Aenergy or BD Alpine Pro)
- Hard shell pants with **full-side zips** (Patagonia M10, Arc'teryx Alpha SV pant)
- **Insulated bibs** (Mountain Hardwear Compressor, Patagonia Macro Puff) — alternative to down suit

## Hand system — 3 gloves + 1 mittens minimum

- 2 × heavy ski/leather gloves (Black Diamond Soloist, Outdoor Research Alti Heated optional)
- 1 × light glove for camp tasks (light wool or fleece)
- 1 × **down expedition mittens** for summit + storms (Black Diamond Mercury Mitt, OR Alti Mitts)
- **Hand-warmer chemistry doesn't work below –25 °F.** Don't rely on them.
- VBL gloves (Latex/vapor barrier) for inside outer mittens summit day

## Head

- Sun hat / ball cap (lower mountain)
- Warm beanie × 2
- Balaclava (mid-weight)
- Neoprene face mask for summit day (anti-frostbite of cheeks/nose)
- **Goggles** — mandatory. Cat 3 or 4 lenses. Smith I/O Mag, OR Spectrum.
- **Glacier glasses** Cat 4 + side shields (Julbo Vermont, Spektrum Östra)
- Spare glasses
- Helmet (Petzl Meteor — fits with balaclava)

## Climbing hardware

- **Crampons:** 12-pt steel, semi-auto/auto for plastic boots, anti-balling plates. Petzl Sarken/Lynx, BD Sabretooth Pro
- **Ice axe:** 60 cm walking axe (Petzl Glacier, BD Raven Pro)
- **Technical second tool (optional):** light technical axe for fixed lines if cracking ice. Most parties skip.
- **Harness:** alpine harness with leg loops over down suit/bib (Petzl Adjama, BD Couloir)
- **Ascender:** Petzl Ascension (right or left) for fixed lines — **mandatory**
- **Belay device:** ATC Guide
- **Pickets × 3** (24"–30" aluminum) per team for snow anchors + camp anchors
- **Ice screws × 4** (10, 13, 16, 22 cm) per team
- **Carabiners:** 6 lockers, 12 standards per person
- **Slings:** 4 × 60 cm dyneema, 2 × 120 cm
- **Cord:** 7 m × 7 mm cord + spare prussiks
- **Prussik cord** pre-tied, on harness (Bachmann or French)
- **Rope:** 1 × 60 m × 8.5 mm dry-treated half rope per team

## Sled / hauling

- **Paris expedition sled** (60") with 2 holes for rope attachment — most common
- OR **CMU pulk** if planning to ski (heavier)
- **Hauling harness:** 2 × bungee cords + waist belt that connects to sled tow lines
- **Tow rope:** 6 m of 5 mm static cord with carabiners both ends — for safety/rescue from crevasse

## Snowshoes / skis

- **Snowshoes:** MSR Lightning Ascent 30" — standard, fits all conditions
- **Skis:** ski mountaineering setup if you ski well; faster on the Kahiltna but heavier and forgiveness lower on crevasse breakthrough

## Tent & sleep system

- **Tent:** 4-season expedition (Hilleberg Keron 3 GT or Black Diamond First Light with vestibule add-on, MSR Access 3)
- **Footprint** (custom-cut Tyvek)
- **Snow stakes ×8** (V-stakes or T-stakes)
- **Snow shovel** (Black Diamond Lynx, Voile XLM) — 1 per person
- **Snow saw** (BCA, Voile) — 1 per team, for building walls
- **Sleeping bag:** –20 to –40 °F rating, down preferred for weight, dry-treated
  - Feathered Friends Snowbunting EX 0 / –20F
  - Western Mountaineering Bison GWS –40
  - Carry a **VBL bag liner** to keep down dry
- **Sleeping pad system:**
  - 1 × full-length closed-cell foam pad (Therm-a-Rest Z-Lite or Switchback)
  - 1 × inflatable insulated pad (NeoAir XTherm NXT, R 7.3) on top
- **Pillow:** stuff sack with clothes
- **Earplugs** (storm noise in tent)

## Stoves / fuel / food

- **Stove × 2** — MSR XGK EX (white gas) for redundancy. White gas only, not iso-butane (too cold above 14k).
- **Stove maintenance kit:** spare pump, jet cleaner, O-rings
- **Pots:** MSR Alpine 1.5 L + 2 L (1 per person, but team-shared cooking)
- **Pot grippers**
- **Spoons** (long-handled titanium)
- **Mugs** (insulated, ~500 ml)
- **Wind screen** (aluminum, fits MSR pots)
- **Fuel:**
  - Estimate **~25–30 oz white gas per person per day** above 14k Camp
  - For 21-day trip: roughly **3 gallons / 12 L total per person**, plus group cooking ~2 gallons extra
  - Buy in Talkeetna — DO NOT fly with fuel
- **Food: 21 days, 3,500–5,500 kcal/day = roughly 75,000+ kcal per person**
  - Pre-packaged: instant oats, freeze-dried dinners (Mountain House, AlpineAire, Backpacker's Pantry), instant noodles, Velveeta+pasta, bagels, salami, cheese, snickers, gummies, energy bars, gels, hot drinks (tea, cocoa, instant coffee, broth)
  - Real fat: butter, olive oil, peanut butter (cold-resistant)
  - Repackage everything in gallon ziplocs by camp-day or by meal
  - **High-altitude appetite suppression:** plan small, calorie-dense, easy-to-stomach items for above 14k (gels, candies, broth, oatmeal)

## Water & sanitation

- 2 × 1L Nalgene wide-mouth bottles (insulated covers)
- 1 × thermos 750 ml (Hydro Flask or similar)
- Chlorine dioxide tabs / drops (Aquatabs) — backup
- **CMC (Clean Mountain Can)** — picked up at ranger station, required for human waste collection above 14k. Plus blue waste bags.
- Pee bottle (1L Nalgene wide-mouth, **marked clearly** with bright tape)
- Female partners: pee funnel
- Hand sanitizer
- Toilet paper in a ziploc

## Personal

- Headlamp Petzl Actik Core / Nao + 4 sets spare batteries (or 2 spare lithium AAAs)
- Watch with altimeter (Suunto, Garmin Fenix)
- Sunscreen mineral SPF 50 (3 × 100 ml tubes for trip)
- Lip balm SPF (×3)
- Toiletries minimal: toothbrush + toothpaste, wipes, hand cream
- Spare contact lenses / glasses + saline
- 2 × dry bags for inside-tent items
- Stuff sacks (organize gear)
- Garbage compactor bags × 5 (waterproofing pack contents)
- Trekking poles (collapsible)
- Repair kit:
  - Multi-tool (Leatherman)
  - Duct tape (wrap 10 ft around poles)
  - Tenacious Tape
  - Spare cord, zip ties, safety pins
  - Spare crampon parts (front bail, strap)
  - Stove maintenance kit (above)
  - Extra batteries

## First aid (expedition)

- Personal Rx + 30% extras + photos of labels
- **Acetazolamide (Diamox)** — 125 mg × 30 tabs prophylactic + treatment
- **Dexamethasone** — 4 mg × 10 tabs (HACE emergency, **doctor-prescribed**)
- **Nifedipine** — 30 mg ER × 10 (HAPE emergency, **doctor-prescribed**)
- Cipro or azithromycin (12 tabs)
- Ibuprofen × 40, Tylenol × 20, Aspirin × 12
- Pepto + Imodium
- Diphenhydramine (sleep, allergy)
- Bandaids, leukotape, athletic tape, 4" elastic wrap, sterile gauze
- Antibiotic ointment, Tegaderm × 4
- Tweezers, small scissors, safety pins
- Hand warmers × 20 (work above 14k Camp until temps drop too far)
- SOL Escape bivy bag (emergency)

## Electronics

- Garmin inReach Mini 2 — mandatory
- Charger / solar panel (Goal Zero Nomad 7 / 10 + Sherpa battery bank) — important for 3 weeks
- Phone (offline maps preloaded — CalTopo + Gaia)
- Battery bank × 1 (10,000–20,000 mAh)
- Extra batteries for headlamp, inReach
- Tape, cable organizer
- Camera (optional — Ricoh GR III or phone-only recommended)
- USB-C cable, microUSB, lightning (whatever your devices need)

## Pack

- **Pack 75–90 L** with frame (Black Diamond Speed Zip, Hyperlite Ice Pack, Gregory Denali — yes, that's the actual name) — must be able to carry 70 lb comfortably
- **Pack liner** (heavy trash compactor bag or HMG cuben fiber pack liner)

## Toiletries / paperwork

- Passport (US citizens technically don't need one but useful)
- Climbing permit confirmation + printout
- Insurance cards (Global Rescue, AAC, primary US health)
- Cash $200 in small bills (for tipping, last-minute Talkeetna)
- Pen + small notebook

## Weight management — what to skip

| Item | Skip if... |
|---|---|
| Down suit | Bringing parka + insulated bibs system |
| Stove × 2 | Solo / strong second of single stove + thorough repair kit (NOT RECOMMENDED) |
| Skis | Don't ski well enough |
| Camera | Not a photographer |
| Extra base layers | Have VBL system |

## Pre-trip checklist (the big one)

**3 months out**
- [ ] Boots broken in (50+ miles, including cold-day hikes)
- [ ] Sled rig tested (load 50 lb on Front Range trail)
- [ ] All shells seam-checked, waterproofed
- [ ] Sleeping bag tested in –20 °F (sleep outside in winter)
- [ ] Stove fully serviced, new O-rings
- [ ] Crampons + axe tuned

**1 month out**
- [ ] All meals pre-packaged by camp-day
- [ ] Repair kit + first aid restocked
- [ ] CMC waste bags ordered (NPS or via guides)
- [ ] Re-watch crevasse rescue videos, drill with team

**Final 2 weeks**
- [ ] Final dry-fit: every layer system tried on with boots/harness/crampons
- [ ] Charge all batteries, check all electronics
- [ ] Confirm Global Rescue active
- [ ] Confirm air taxi booking + pickup time
