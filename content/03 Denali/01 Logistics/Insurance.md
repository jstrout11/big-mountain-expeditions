# Insurance — Denali

**Alaska Range medevac runs $100k+.** Standard travel insurance does not cover technical climbing above 4,500–6,000 m or helicopter rescue. You need a specialized stack.

## Recommended stack

### 1. Global Rescue Annual — primary evacuation
- **Cost:** ~$700/year individual
- **Covers:** technical mountaineering to any elevation, **no per-incident cap** on evacuation costs
- **Best for:** the actual scenario that ends the trip — needing to be lifted off the mountain or out of Alaska
- [globalrescue.com](https://www.globalrescue.com/)

### 2. AAC Rescue Benefit — secondary layer
- **Cost:** $95/year membership
- **Covers:** up to **$15,000 SAR** + **$5,000 medical** worldwide
- **Best for:** belt-and-suspenders on the SAR side; also pays for AAC publications + Climbers Ranch access

### 3. Primary US health insurance — medical side
- Verify your plan covers **Providence Hospital Anchorage** (the main trauma center) and **out-of-network helicopter transport**
- Most plans do cover emergency transport but require pre-approval — call before the trip and document

`denali_insurance:: Global Rescue + AAC + primary US health plan`
`primary_hospital:: Providence Hospital Anchorage`

## Things to verify on Global Rescue

- [ ] **Activity coverage:** "technical mountaineering" — Denali at 6,000 m+ is explicitly included
- [ ] **Altitude:** no cap on standard alpine activities
- [ ] **Solo coverage:** if going solo, confirm — some policies exclude solo (Global Rescue doesn't, but verify your tier)
- [ ] **Annual auto-renew** set so you don't lapse mid-trip

## What rescue actually costs

| Service | Cost |
|---|---:|
| NPS SAR helicopter (true emergency) | $0 (federal SAR mandate) |
| Non-SAR helicopter (e.g., evacuated due to HAPE but ambulatory) | **$10,000–40,000** |
| Hospital transport Talkeetna → Anchorage | $5–20k |
| Medevac to Lower 48 | **$100k+** |
| Hospital admission Providence | $5,000+/day |

> [!warning] The non-SAR helicopter case
> The NPS will SAR you for free **if it's a true emergency**. But moves like "evacuated due to HAPE but ambulatory" can fall outside the SAR mandate — that's when private medevac costs hit. **This is the scenario Global Rescue exists for.**

## Pre-trip checklist

- [ ] Global Rescue annual active, paperwork in pack
- [ ] AAC membership current
- [ ] Primary US health insurance: call to verify Providence Anchorage in network or have out-of-network emergency policy understood
- [ ] Print insurance cards (laminated copy) + carry digital photos
- [ ] List of emergency contacts (next of kin, family physician, team partners) on Garmin inReach
