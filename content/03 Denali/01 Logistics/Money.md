# Money — Denali

Budget envelopes per person (USD):

| Tier | Cost |
|---|---:|
| **Unguided cheap** (have most gear) | ~$4,500 |
| **Unguided realistic** | ~$6,500 |
| **Unguided comfortable** (buy/upgrade gear) | ~$9,000+ |
| **Guided** | $11,500–14,500+ |

## Guided variant

- **AAI / Alpine Ascents / RMI / Mountain Trip:** $11,500–14,500/person for the standard 21-day West Buttress program
- **What's included:** guides, group gear (tents, ropes, fuel, group meals), permit fees, transportation Anchorage-Kahiltna round trip
- **What's NOT included:** personal gear, flights to Anchorage, lodging Anchorage / Talkeetna, tips ($800–1,500 typical)
- See [[Guides & Operators]]

## Gear-cost shock for first-timers

| Item | Cost |
|---|---:|
| **8000 m double boots** (Olympus Mons / Phantom 8000) | $1,000 |
| **Heavyweight down parka** (FF Volant / Rab Expedition) | $700–900 |
| **–40 °F sleeping bag** (FF Snowbunting / WM Bison) | $800–1,200 |
| **Expedition tent** (Hilleberg / BD First Light) | $700–1,000 |
| **Down expedition mittens** (BD Mercury Mitt / OR Alti) | $200 |
| **Glacier glasses Cat 4** (Julbo Vermont) | $200 |
| **Other specialty:** snowshoes, sled rig, pickets, extra fuel bottles | $400–600 |
| **Total new-gear hit (first Denali)** | **$4,000–5,000** |

If you have most of this from prior trips (Rainier, etc.), gear depreciation is closer to $500–1,000 per Denali trip. **First time = expect to spend $3,000–5,000 on new gear.**

## Cards & cash

- US domestic — easy. But once you're on the glacier, **money is meaningless**. Pre-pay everything.
- Bring cash for air taxi tips ($50–100 per pilot is standard if you had a hassle-free flight in and out)
- Bring small bills for Talkeetna — Roadhouse, gear last-minute, etc.
- $200–300 in small bills total for the trip

## Tipping conventions

| Service | Tip |
|---|---|
| Air taxi pilot (no issues) | $50–100 cash per pilot |
| Air taxi pilot (heroic weather decision) | $100–200 |
| Guided trip total (split among guides) | $800–1,500 (~10% of fee) |
| Talkeetna Roadhouse breakfast | 18–20% (it's a real restaurant) |

## Money safety rules

- Two cards (credit + debit) in separate places
- Cash separated from cards
- Photos of all card numbers + insurance cards stored in Garmin inReach memos
- Power of attorney / emergency contact set up before trip — long radio silence on glacier means others may need to act on your behalf
