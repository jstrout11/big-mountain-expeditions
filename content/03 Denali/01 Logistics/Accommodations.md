# Accommodations — Denali

## Anchorage (transit nights)

- **Pre-climb:** stay near the airport or downtown for the gear/food shop. Budget: Black Spruce, Earth B&B, Sourdough Lodge. Mid-range: Hilton, Hyatt.
- **AMH (Alaska Mountaineering & Hiking)** — gear shop in Anchorage. Stop here for last-minute fuel, stove parts, fresh batteries. [AMH website](https://www.alaskamountaineering.com/)

`anchorage_default_nights:: 1 in + 1 out`

## Talkeetna (1–3 nights minimum)

- **Required:** in-person check-in at the **Walter Harper Talkeetna Ranger Station** before flying in
- **Talkeetna Roadhouse** — climbing institution. Bunkhouse rooms ~$30–50, private rooms $100+, breakfast is famous. Book ahead, very popular. [Talkeetna Roadhouse](https://talkeetnaroadhouse.com/)
- **Latitude 62 Lodge** — alternative, cheaper, bare-bones
- **Air taxi cabins:** TAT and K2 both have lodge / bunkhouse options for clients while waiting on weather

`talkeetna_default_nights:: 2 pre + 1-3 post (buffer)`
`talkeetna_lodging_default:: Talkeetna Roadhouse`

## On the mountain — 8 camps over 17–21 days

| Camp | Elevation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Base camp (SE Fork Kahiltna) | 7,200 ft / 2,200 m | Fly-in point. Camp here only the night you arrive. |
| Ski Hill camp | 7,800 ft / 2,380 m | Most teams skip — push directly to Camp 1 |
| **Camp 1 (Kahiltna Pass)** | 7,800 ft / 2,380 m | Cache and move day, 5 mi from base |
| **Camp 2** | 9,500 ft / 2,900 m | "11 Camp" approach, common cache stop |
| **Camp 3 — 11,200 Camp** | 11,200 ft / 3,400 m | First major camp. Most spend 1 acclimatization day. |
| **Camp 4 — 14,200 Camp (Genet Basin)** | 14,200 ft / 4,330 m | The acclimatization base. **Plan 4–7 days here.** Cache on fixed lines, rest day, push to high camp when weather + body align. |
| **Camp 5 — 17,200 / High Camp** | 17,200 ft / 5,240 m | Cold, windy. Summit from here. Don't sit more than 2 nights — burning oxygen, getting weaker. |
| Summit | 20,310 ft / 6,190 m | |

## Camp building (critical skill on the mountain)

- **Snow walls:** quarry blocks 50 × 30 × 20 cm with snow saw, stack to wind-block
- **Tent platform:** stomp out flat area 3 × 3 m, let cure 30 min before pitching
- **Anchor system:** every guyline + main stake = picket, T-slot, or carabiner-clipped axe
- **Vestibule for cooking + storage**
- **Pee hole** uphill from tent, downwind
- **CMC location** downwind, marked, 30 m from tent

## Caching strategy

Standard pattern: **"carry high, sleep low."** Move loads up, return to lower camp, sleep, then move camp up the next day. Adds days but the acclimatization payoff is huge.

- **Cache between every camp pair** below 14k
- Wand all caches (bamboo wand w/ flag) — visible 100m+
- Bury caches if leaving > 1 day
- Store fuel containers visible, separate from food

## Booking timing

- Air taxi: 4–6 months ahead
- Talkeetna Roadhouse: 3–4 months ahead for May/June
- Anchorage hotel: 2–3 months ahead
