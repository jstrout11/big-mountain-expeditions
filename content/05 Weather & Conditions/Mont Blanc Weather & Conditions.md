# Mont Blanc Weather & Conditions

## Climbing season

| Month | Climbable | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| May | No / early-season | Heavy snow, late storms; serac stability still building |
| **June (mid → end)** | **Yes** | Long days, snowy conditions, less crowded |
| **July** | **Yes** | Peak season — crowded refuges, generally stable |
| **August** | **Yes** | Peak; later August heat spikes can destabilize seracs |
| **September (early-mid)** | **Yes — often best window** | Cooler, fewer crowds, stable high-pressure days |
| September (late) | Marginal | Refuges close mid-late September; winter onset |
| Oct–May | No | Winter / spring conditions |

`mont_blanc_target_window:: late June OR early September`

> [!info] The late-summer caveat
> 2010s and 2020s have seen recurring **heat waves** in late July / August destabilizing seracs on Mont Blanc du Tacul. Local guides have periodically closed Trois Monts during extreme heat. **Check La Chamoniarde before committing.**

## Primary forecast sources

- **[Météo France Mont Blanc / Chamonix bulletin](https://meteofrance.com/meteo-montagne/massif-mont-blanc/MASSIF010)**: official mountain bulletin, free, **the** standard for the French side. Updated 16:00 daily for next day.
- **[Météo Chamonix detail](https://meteofrance.com/previsions-meteo-france/chamonix-mont-blanc/74400)**: valley-station forecast
- **[Mountain Forecast Mont Blanc](https://www.mountain-forecast.com/peaks/Mont-Blanc/forecasts/4810)**: elevation-resolved (good for summit-ridge planning)
- **[MeteoBlue Mont Blanc](https://www.meteoblue.com/en/weather/forecast/multimodel/mont-blanc_france_3181499)**: multi-model comparison, useful for understanding spread
- **[Yr.no Mont Blanc](https://www.yr.no/place/France/Rh%C3%B4ne-Alpes/Mont_Blanc/)**: Norwegian service, free, alpine-relevant
- **[La Chamoniarde](https://www.chamoniarde.com/conditions)**: aggregated route-by-route conditions, posted weekly — **best source for objective hazard updates** (serac instability, snow conditions, fixed equipment state)
- **[Office de Haute Montagne (OHM)](https://www.ohm-chamonix.com/)**: weather + route conditions, English-friendly

## Climbing-weather priorities

1. **Wind at the summit** — sustained 50+ km/h on the summit ridge makes Trois Monts ungoable. Check 4,800 m wind forecast.
2. **Cloud / freezing level** — below 4,000 m freezing = snow on the climbing. Above 4,500 m = warm snow underfoot, melting seracs above.
3. **Precipitation** — any precip in last 48 h = increased avalanche risk on Maudit slope + serac instability above Tacul
4. **Visibility** — Mont Maudit slope and the Maudit Col are tough to navigate in white-out
5. **Lightning** — convective afternoon storms June–August on the south side. Be at refuge by 14:00, not above 4,000 m.

## Reading the Météo France Mont Blanc bulletin

The official bulletin uses a stoplight system + key parameters:

- **Iso 0°C (freezing level)** — typical July is 4,200–4,500 m. Lower = colder climb, more snow; higher = warmer, serac concerns.
- **Vent au sommet** — wind at summit, in km/h. Threshold: <30 km/h ideal, 30–50 climbable but uncomfortable, 50+ no-go.
- **Risque d'orage** (thunderstorm risk) — none / faible (low) / modéré (moderate) / fort (strong) — anything above faible = scrub
- **Visibilité** — bonne (good) / moyenne (moderate) / mauvaise (poor)
- **Verglas / neige fraîche** — ice or fresh snow on route

## Practical decision framework

| Forecast feature | Action |
|---|---|
| Freezing level 4,500 m, wind <30 km/h summit, no precip 48 h, stable high pressure | **Send.** This is the ideal Trois Monts day. |
| Freezing level 4,800+ m, recent warmth | **Hazard check on serac stability** — call OHM/Chamoniarde first |
| Wind 50+ km/h summit | No-go on Trois Monts; consider Cosmiques Arête or rest day |
| Storm risk modéré or above | No-go for upper mountain |
| Recent snow 20+ cm | Wait 48 h minimum for avalanche risk on Maudit; verify with guide office |
| 3+ day high-pressure window | Schedule the climb confidently |

## Avalanche

- **Avalanche bulletin:** [Météo France Mont-Blanc Massif bulletin](https://meteofrance.com/meteo-montagne/massif-mont-blanc/MASSIF010) — 5-level European scale (1–5)
- **Above 3 (Marked)** = scrub Trois Monts; Maudit slope is the avalanche-prone section
- **Spring snowpack instability** persists into June; **late summer fresh snow** = high reactivity
- **Wind slab** common on Maudit slope after windy days — local Chamonix guides will know

## Route-specific objective hazards (timing)

- **Mont Blanc du Tacul serac:** safest before sunrise (typically before 06:00 local time). After 08:00 in heat, risk climbs.
- **Maudit slope:** safest in cold, dark conditions. After sun hits (typically 09:00–10:00), snow softens and seracs above warm.
- **Glacier crossings:** crevasse bridges thinnest mid-afternoon. Get back to refuge or down by 14:00.

## Conditions sources

- **[La Chamoniarde "Conditions"](https://www.chamoniarde.com/conditions)**: weekly route updates — *the* authoritative source
- **[OHM bulletin board](https://www.ohm-chamonix.com/)**: live conditions board in the Chamonix office; visit in person if possible
- **[Camptocamp Mont Blanc routes](https://www.camptocamp.org/routes#a=339&t=r&w=mass2010)**: climber-uploaded recent ticks with photos
- **[r/alpinism subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/alpinism/)**: occasional Mont Blanc condition reports
- **Refuge phone calls:** Refuge des Cosmiques staff get questioned about conditions daily; if you speak French, call ahead

## Pre-departure check (48 h before fly)

- [ ] Météo France 4-day bulletin shows stable window
- [ ] La Chamoniarde conditions show route open + no serac warning
- [ ] Refuge des Cosmiques availability confirmed (your booking + climbing partner)
- [ ] Backup plan if window closes: Cosmiques Arête, Gran Paradiso, Italian-side objective
