# Insurance — Mont Blanc

**French helicopter rescue is billed to non-EU climbers** (€5,000–15,000 typical). Standard US travel insurance generally excludes technical alpine climbing. You need a policy specifically covering it.

## Recommended stack

### Option A — ÖAV Alpenverein (cheapest, very effective)
- **Austrian Alpenverein** membership includes the **Alpenverein-Weltweit-Service** — worldwide alpine rescue + repatriation up to **€25,000**, **no altitude cap on standard alpine activities**
- **Cost:** ~€75/year
- Many European refuges discount with ÖAV card (small but real perk)
- [alpenverein.at international page](https://www.alpenverein.at/portal_en/index.php)

### Option B — Global Rescue + travel medical
- **Global Rescue Annual:** ~$700/year, **no per-incident cap** on evacuation
- Pair with primary travel medical (IMG Patriot Platinum, GeoBlue) for medical bills
- Overkill for Mont Blanc alone, but worth it if Denali is also on the horizon

### Option C — Standalone travel medical w/ adventure rider
- World Nomads "Explorer Plan" — confirm "mountaineering up to 6000 m"
- Cheaper option for one-off trips

`mont_blanc_insurance:: ÖAV Alpenverein (recommended) OR Global Rescue`
`primary_hospital:: Hôpital de Chamonix-Mont-Blanc / Hôpital de Sallanches`

## What rescue actually costs in Chamonix

| Service | Cost |
|---|---:|
| PGHM helicopter pickup (foreigner) | €5,000–15,000 |
| Hôpital Chamonix admission | €1,000–3,000 |
| Repatriation flight US | €30,000–60,000 |

## Things to verify on your policy

- [ ] **Activity coverage:** "mountaineering" or "alpinism" explicitly named — not just "trekking" or "hiking"
- [ ] **Altitude cap:** above 4,808 m (some policies cap at 4,500 m)
- [ ] **Helicopter evac:** explicitly covered, no "medical necessity" gatekeeper clause
- [ ] **Repatriation:** transport home from France covered
- [ ] **Pre-existing conditions:** disclosed honestly
- [ ] **Glacier travel + steep snow climbing covered** — Maudit slope is well within "alpine climbing"

## What standard travel policies miss

- "Adventure sport" or "hazardous activity" exclusions that quietly void coverage above some elevation (often 4,000–6,000 m)
- No helicopter evac, or evac only "if medically necessary as determined by the insurer" (i.e., not when *you* need it)
- $50k–$100k caps that are too low for repatriation
