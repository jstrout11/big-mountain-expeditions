# Permits & Registration — Mont Blanc

## No permit required to climb

- France imposes **no climbing permit** on Mont Blanc itself
  - This is a recent and ongoing debate — the **Saint-Gervais mayor** has periodically tried to require one for the **Goûter route** in summer
  - **Trois Monts is not affected** by these proposals
- The mountain is open. You can walk up tomorrow if you have the skills and the weather.

## What you do need to book

### Refuge du Cosmiques
- Most-booked refuge in the Alps in summer. Opens ~6 months out, fills fast for Jul–Aug weekends.
- See [[Accommodations]] for details.

### Aiguille du Midi téléphérique
- Can be reserved online to avoid 1–2h summer queue. [Compagnie du Mont Blanc](https://www.montblancnaturalresort.com/)

### PGHM Chamonix (mountain rescue)
- The Peloton de Gendarmerie de Haute Montagne is the mountain rescue service.
- **No registration required**, but it's strongly recommended to:
  - Leave a route plan at your refuge or with your accommodation
  - Carry the [SafeMountain Chamonix](https://chamonix.safemountain.fr/en) app — has route conditions and emergency reporting
- **PGHM Chamonix phone: +33 4 50 53 16 89**

## Documents to carry

- **Passport** — French entry, carry on body
- **Travel insurance card with helicopter evac coverage** — French helicopter rescue is free for residents/EU but **billable to foreigners**. Standard rescue $5,000–15,000. See [[Insurance]].
- **ÖAV / AAC card if you have one** — discount at refuges, secondary rescue coverage
