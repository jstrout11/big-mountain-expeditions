## Guided variants (if reconsidering)

- **Single skills / acclimatization day with Chamonix guide:** €400–600 (excellent value-add)
- **Full guided Trois Monts 2-3 day:** €2,500–3,200 (1:1 or 1:2)
- **4-day prep + Trois Monts course:** €2,000–2,500

## Cards & cash

- **Card-first:** France is heavily cards. Visa/MC accepted nearly everywhere including refuges.
- **Get a card with no foreign-transaction fee** (Chase Sapphire, Capital One Venture, Schwab debit) — typical 3% FTF adds up
- **Refuge wallet:** ~€50 small notes for shared refuge purchases (extra meal, beer)
- **PIN-required:** some unattended self-service kiosks require chip+PIN — confirm your card has a PIN before traveling

## Tipping conventions

| Service | Tip |
|---|---|
| Single-day guide (skills clinic) | 10–15% of fee |
| Multi-day guided climb | 5–10% of fee |
| Refuge staff | Not expected; round up at bar |
| Shuttle driver | €5–10 per leg |
| Restaurant | Service is included; €1–5 for good service |

## Money safety rules

- Carry **one credit card + one debit card + €100 cash** in two separate pockets/bags
- ÖAV card or AAC card in wallet
- Insurance policy number / Global Rescue card
