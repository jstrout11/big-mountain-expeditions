# Travel & Flights — Mont Blanc

Home airport is **DEN**. Closest major airport to Chamonix is **GVA (Geneva)**.

## Recommended routing

- **DEN → GVA (Geneva)** is the headline routing — closest major airport to Chamonix
  - United DEN→ZRH→GVA, Air France DEN→CDG→GVA, Lufthansa DEN→FRA→GVA, KLM DEN→AMS→GVA
  - Round trip ~$900–1,500 economy depending on season; July–Aug is peak
- **GVA → Chamonix:** ~88 km, ~1h 15m
  - Shared shuttle: [Mountain Drop-offs](https://www.mountaindropoffs.com/), [AlpyBus](https://www.alpybus.com/), [SAT Chamonix](https://www.sat-montblanc.com/) — ~€35–50 each way, must pre-book
  - Private taxi: €200–250 each way
  - Train: GVA → St-Gervais → Chamonix on Mont Blanc Express, ~3h with transfer, ~€30

## Alternative routings

- **DEN → ZRH (Zurich)** — sometimes cheaper, drive 4h to Chamonix or train 6h with transfers
- **DEN → CDG (Paris)** — long train to Chamonix (~6h via Lyon), rarely better unless paired with Paris stop
- **DEN → LYS (Lyon)** — closer than CDG but fewer transatlantic options

## Flight-day rules

> [!tip] Carry on the boots
> Always carry on double mountaineering boots and harness/helmet. Checked-bag mishandling can end the trip. Pack a small duffel inside checked luggage for the climb (lockers at Chamonix accommodation handle the rest).

- **Carry on:** boots, harness, helmet, prescription meds, key insulation layer, headlamp
- **Check:** pack, sleeping bag (refuge has bedding so this is light/optional), tents (not needed), hardware (rack confiscated as carry-on — must be checked)
- **Fuel:** never fly with fuel canisters or white gas. Refuge handles all meals; no stove needed.
- **Lithium batteries:** carry-on only

## Local transport in Chamonix

- **Téléphérique to Aiguille du Midi:** ~€75 round-trip. Book online to skip 1–2h summer queue. [Compagnie du Mont Blanc](https://www.montblancnaturalresort.com/)
- **Mont Blanc Express train** runs Le Châtelard → Vallorcine → Chamonix → Servoz → St-Gervais — free with Chamonix Carte d'Hôte (guest card from accommodation)
- **Chamonix → St-Gervais (descent route from Goûter)** — train or shuttle; plan ~2h post-descent
- **Nid d'Aigle Tramway** descent option — takes you from 2,386 m to St-Gervais. ~€36 one-way.
