# Guides & Operators — Mont Blanc

Vault default is **unguided**, but **guides are the default for most parties on Trois Monts**. The Maudit slope + objective hazard makes IFMGA guidance an entirely reasonable call even for experienced climbers on a first Mont Blanc.

## Compagnie des Guides de Chamonix

- The oldest mountain guides company in the world (founded 1821)
- IFMGA-certified guides only
- **Programs:**
  - **Guided Mont Blanc Trois Monts:** 2-3 day program, **€2,500–3,200** (1:1 or 1:2)
  - **Mont Blanc preparation course:** 4-day program, €2,000–2,500 — includes Aiguille du Tour + Cosmiques climb + Trois Monts
  - **Single-day acclimatization / skills with guide:** **€400–600** — see decision framework below
- [chamonix-guides.com](https://www.chamonix-guides.com/)

## Chamex

- Aggregator + independent guides
- Often easier to find English-speaking guide with availability
- [chamex.com](https://www.chamex.com/)

## Argentière Guides Office

- Smaller, valley-based collective in Argentière (5 km north of Chamonix)
- IFMGA guides at competitive rates
- [guides-argentiere.com](https://www.guides-argentiere.com/)

## Other notable European operators

- **Adventure Consultants (NZ-based, runs Mont Blanc programs):** [adventureconsultants.com](https://www.adventureconsultants.com/)
- **Alpenglow Expeditions (US, runs Alps trips):** [alpenglowexpeditions.com](https://www.alpenglowexpeditions.com/)

## Decision framework

| Situation | Recommended approach |
|---|---|
| **Doing Trois Monts unguided, prior glacier + 4,000 m experience** | **Reasonable with prep.** Strongly consider a **1-day pre-climb skills/acclimatization day with a Chamonix guide** for ~€500 — best value-add of any guide service across the three trips. |
| First glacier trip / first major alpine route | Guide the whole thing. €2,500–3,200 is fair for the safety margin. |
| Solo (no partner) | Hire a 1:1 guide or join a small group from one of the operators above |
| No crevasse rescue experience | Either: (a) hire guide; or (b) defer trip 6 months and take a course |

## Single-day guide use cases

A guided **Cosmiques Arête day** (PD, 2h climb + transit) is the highest-value short option:
- €400–500 for ~5 h total
- Practice glacier travel + short-roping + steep snow + summit a small peak
- Get current beta on Trois Monts conditions from someone who climbs it weekly
- Excellent acclimatization (sleep Cosmiques same night)

## How to evaluate any guide service

- ✅ **IFMGA certified guides** — non-negotiable for Trois Monts
- ✅ **Liability insurance + permit compliance** — every Chamonix-licensed guide is fine
- ✅ **Guide-to-client ratio appropriate to objective** — 1:1 or 1:2 max for Trois Monts
- ✅ **Recent successful trip reports / reviews** — TripAdvisor, AAC forums
- ✅ **Conservative weather decision-making track record** — ask about turn-around stories
- ✅ **Clear gear / fitness expectations communicated in advance**
- ⚠️ **Beware:** newer outfits without IFMGA certification, "value" pricing well below competitors, vague cancellation policies

## Tipping conventions

| Service | Tip |
|---|---|
| Single-day guide (skills clinic) | 10–15% of fee (~€40–80) |
| Multi-day guided Trois Monts | 5–10% of fee (~€150–300) |
