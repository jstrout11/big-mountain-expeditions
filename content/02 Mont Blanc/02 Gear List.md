# Gear List — Mont Blanc

Target: **summit-day pack 40–45 L, 10–12 kg**. Refuge handles bedding, dinner, breakfast — meaningful weight savings vs Denali.

## Footwear (the single most important call)

### Option A — Single-skin mountaineering boot (recommended for summer)
- **La Sportiva Nepal Cube GTX**, **Scarpa Mont Blanc Pro GTX**, **Salewa Pro Gaiter** — single-skin insulated, B3 crampon compatible
- Warm enough for –20 °C summit day in fair weather (which is the only time you should be there)
- Better walking comfort than 8000m doubles, fewer blisters in the Aig du Midi descent

### Option B — Light double boot (for shoulder season / cold-snap years)
- **Scarpa Phantom Tech HD**, **La Sportiva G2 Evo** — modern lightweight doubles
- Overkill in July–August unless temps unusually cold
- Better margin if forecast says –25 °C summit day

**For unguided Trois Monts in July/August: Option A is standard.**

## Climbing hardware

- **Crampons:** 12-point steel, semi-automatic or automatic binding (Petzl Vasak/Lynx, BD Sabretooth Pro). Anti-balling plates **mandatory** in summer wet snow.
- **Ice axe:** 50–55 cm walking axe with classic pick (Petzl Glacier or Snowwalker, BD Raven Pro)
- **Harness:** light alpine (Petzl Sitta or BD Couloir)
- **Helmet** (Petzl Meteor or Sirocco)
- **Glacier rope:** 30–40 m, 8.5–9 mm half rope — one rope per team of 2–3
- **Crevasse rescue kit:**
  - 2 × prussiks (5 mm × 1.5 m and × 2.2 m) on harness, pre-tied
  - 2 × locking carabiners
  - 1 × pulley (Petzl Microtraxion ideal — also doubles as progress capture)
  - 1 × tibloc or ropeman (small ascender for hauling)
  - 2 × 120 cm sewn slings
  - 2 × ice screws (16 cm, 13 cm)
  - 1 × snow picket (or 1 per team — pickets are mainly for the Maudit slope)
- **Light rack** (Maudit slope sometimes needs):
  - 4 × alpine draws
  - 2 × 60 cm slings
  - 2 × screws (above)
  - 1 × small cam (BD 0.4 + 0.5) — rare use, but lightweight

## Clothing — summit day (it gets cold at 4800 m at 4 a.m.)

- Synthetic or merino base layer top
- Mid-weight insulating layer (R1 hoodie or Patagonia Nano-Air Light Hybrid)
- **Soft shell jacket** — kept on most of the climb (Arc'teryx Gamma LT, Patagonia R1 TechFace)
- Hard shell jacket (Gore-Tex Pro, Arc'teryx Beta AR, Patagonia Triolet)
- Mid-weight down or synthetic belay puffy — must fit OVER hard shell (Patagonia DAS Light, Rab Microlight Alpine)
- Base layer pants (light wool or synthetic)
- Soft shell pants (Mammut Aenergy, Black Diamond Alpine)
- Hard shell pants with full-side zips (Patagonia M10, Arc'teryx Beta AR pant)
- Wool buff or balaclava
- Beanie (warm wool/synthetic blend)
- Sun hat or ball cap (refuge)
- **Two pairs of gloves:**
  - Light insulated glove for climbing (Black Diamond Mont Blanc, Outdoor Research Lodestar)
  - Warm insulated glove or light mitt for cold sections / belay (Black Diamond Soloist, BD Mercury Mitt)
- Wool ski socks × 2 pair + 1 light liner

## Refuge & approach

- Light hiking pants / approach pant
- T-shirt or short-sleeve base
- Approach shoes for the téléphérique → Cosmiques walk (some prefer to wear boots — your call, walking poles help either way)
- Camp shoes / Crocs / hut slippers (refuge required — outside boots stay at door)
- Towel + small toiletries
- Refuge requires earplugs (snoring dorm) and eye mask
- Lightweight sleeping bag liner (refuge bedding is washed but a liner is nicer)
- €50 small bills for refuge dinner / extras

## Stove / food (lightweight, refuge does main meals)

- **No stove needed** — refuge dinner + breakfast
- Day snacks: 1500–2000 cal for summit day
  - Energy bars × 4–6 (Cliff, Honey Stinger waffles)
  - Snickers × 3
  - Gels × 6 (Maurten or Honey Stinger)
  - Salami stick / cheese
  - Hard candies (energy + dry-mouth relief)
- Optional small thermos with hot drink for summit

## Water

- 2 × 1 L Nalgene (insulated covers if you have them) or hard plastic bottles
- Aquatabs (refuge water is potable but iodine in pack as backup)
- Optional: **lightweight stove + 1 fuel canister** for melting snow on a long summit day — discussed at refuge; most parties skip

## Personal

- **Glacier glasses Cat 4 with side shields** (Julbo Vermont Classic, Spektrum Östra) — **non-negotiable**
- Spare sunglasses (cheap, in case primary breaks)
- Sunscreen mineral SPF 50 + lip balm SPF
- Headlamp Petzl Actik Core / Nao + 1 spare battery set
- Phone + waterproof case
- Wallet + cards (PIN-enabled)
- Passport (carry on body)
- Insurance card (Global Rescue / ÖAV)
- Refuge confirmation printout

## Electronics

- Phone with SafeMountain Chamonix app installed
- Garmin inReach Mini 2 (SOS + tracking) — optional but recommended
- Watch with altimeter
- Battery bank (10,000 mAh, USB-C)
- All cables in small zip case

## First aid (alpine kit)

- Ibuprofen × 20, Tylenol × 10, Aspirin × 8 (HACE emergency)
- Acetazolamide × 8 (optional; see [[Health & Acclimatization]])
- Imodium × 4
- Bandaids, leukotape (blisters), athletic tape
- 3" elastic wrap
- Antibiotic ointment
- Personal Rx + spare contacts
- Small whistle
- Emergency bivy bag (SOL Escape Pro)

## Pack list — what stays at refuge, what goes summit

**Stay at refuge:**
- Hiking pants, approach shoes, t-shirt
- Camp shoes
- Toiletries, towel
- Refuge bag/liner (if used)

**Summit pack (40–45 L):**
- All climbing hardware
- All summit-day clothing (worn or in pack)
- 2 L water + snacks + thermos
- First aid
- Sunglasses + spare
- Headlamp + spare batteries
- Phone, inReach, battery
- Sunscreen, lip balm
- Light gloves on hands, warm gloves clipped to harness
- Hard shell stuffed in lid pocket

## Pre-trip checklist

- [ ] Boots fit with summit-day sock combo, broken in (5+ days hiking)
- [ ] Crampons fit boots and tested (anti-balls clean and attached)
- [ ] Crevasse rescue: practiced full haul with partner in last 90 days
- [ ] Helmet not expired
- [ ] Glasses Cat 4 confirmed (not Cat 3)
- [ ] Insurance card with helicopter coverage in wallet
- [ ] Refuge des Cosmiques booking confirmed
- [ ] PGHM number saved in phone
