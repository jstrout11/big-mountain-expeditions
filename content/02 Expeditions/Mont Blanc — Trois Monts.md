# Mont Blanc — Trois Monts

The most beautiful way up Mont Blanc — across the *Three Mountains* (Mont Blanc du Tacul, Mont Maudit, Mont Blanc) from the Aiguille du Midi side. **Harder than the Goûter route, less crowded, more committing**, with serac/avalanche objective hazard on the Maudit slope. The connoisseur's line.

`route_grade:: PD+ / AD-`
`length_summit_day_hours:: 10-14`
`elevation_gain_ft:: 3950 from Cosmiques`
`approach:: téléphérique to 3777m, walk to Cosmiques`
`season_window:: mid-June to mid-September (narrow)`

## Why Trois Monts (vs Goûter)

| Route | Grade | Crowds | Objective hazard | Aesthetic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Trois Monts** | PD+ / AD- | low-medium | **serac + crevasse** | spectacular |
| **Goûter (Voie Normale)** | F+ / PD | very high | **Grand Couloir rockfall** | utilitarian |

The Goûter is the "easier" route by grade but the **Grand Couloir** — a steep gully crossed twice — is statistically the most dangerous spot on Mont Blanc (people are killed by rockfall there most summers). The route has also faced periodic local permit threats.

Trois Monts trades the Grand Couloir for a more technical line and the seracs above the Col du Mont Maudit. **Different hazard profile, similar overall risk, much better climb.**

## Route description

### Day 1 — arrive Chamonix, settle
- Fly DEN → GVA → bus to Chamonix
- Sleep low (Chamonix ~1,035 m)
- Visit [La Chamoniarde](https://www.chamoniarde.com/) — the local mountain info bureau, free, gives current conditions on every standard route. Ask specifically about the Maudit slope.
- Visit [OHM — Office de Haute Montagne](https://www.ohm-chamonix.com/) — refuge bookings, route topos
- Confirm Cosmiques reservation, last-minute weather check (see [[Mont Blanc Weather & Conditions]])

### Day 2 — acclimatization
- **Aiguille du Tour (3,540 m)** day climb from Albert Premier hut — gold-standard warm-up. Some teams do two nights at Albert Premier.
- **Or:** Petit Aiguille Verte (3,512 m) — half-day from Plan de l'Aiguille téléphérique stop, more accessible, less acclimatization gain
- **Or:** chain it — Aiguille du Tour Day 2 + sleep Cosmiques Day 3, summit Mont Blanc Day 4
- If time-constrained: skip dedicated acclimatization day, go straight to Cosmiques and accept harder summit day

### Day 3 — Chamonix → Aiguille du Midi → Refuge des Cosmiques
- **First téléphérique:** 06:30 (verify seasonal schedule)
- Top of Aiguille du Midi: **3,777 m** — already feeling the altitude. Don't move fast.
- **Arête du Midi descent:** 30 min exposed snow ridge from upper station down to the *glacier du Géant*. Crampons + harness + short rope. Drops ~150 m of exposure on both sides — narrow.
- Walk across glacier ~30 min to Refuge des Cosmiques (3,613 m)
- **Afternoon at refuge:** rest, drink water, gear sort, study route, eat, asleep by 19:00
- **Dinner is at a fixed time** (usually 18:30) — don't miss it
- Many parties do the **Cosmiques Arête** (PD, 2h) as a final shake-out the afternoon they arrive. Excellent option if you arrive by noon.

### Day 4 — Summit day
**The big one. Wake 00:30, on the trail by 01:30.**

| Stage | Altitude | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Cosmiques start | 3,613 m | Headlamps, crampons, rope team of 2–3 |
| Col du Midi | 3,613 m | Flat glacier walk ~30 min |
| **Mont Blanc du Tacul (4,248 m)** | 4,248 m | ~2.5 h climb up 40° snow slope. **Serac hazard from above** — move fast, no resting in fall line. **Bergschrund** at base sometimes tricky. Then descend ~150 m the other side. |
| Col Maudit | 4,035 m | The save point — flat glacier basin |
| **Mont Maudit slope** | 4,300–4,468 m | The crux. 45–55° snow/ice slope ~150 m, fixed rope often in place. **The objective hazard scene:** seracs above, deep crevasses below. Move quickly. |
| Col du Mont Maudit | 4,345 m | Top of crux — heart rate down, breathe |
| **Mont Blanc summit ridge** | 4,400–4,808 m | Long fatigue-test up the snow ridge to the top. ~2 h, gradual but at altitude. |
| **Mont Blanc summit (4,808 m)** | 4,808 m | **Target: 08:00–10:00.** Spend 10 min, photo, then immediately start down. |

### Day 4 (continued) — descent
- **Standard descent: Goûter route** down through Vallot hut (4,362 m) → Aiguille du Goûter (3,835 m) → Tête Rousse (3,167 m) → Nid d'Aigle (2,386 m) → tramway to St-Gervais → train to Chamonix
- **Why descend Goûter:** crossing Tacul + Maudit late afternoon = much higher serac risk than morning. Reversing Trois Monts late is *bad practice*.
- **Time:** Mont Blanc summit → Chamonix valley **8–11 hours**. Long day.
- **Alternative descent (less common):** reverse Trois Monts if you summit very early and weather/serac conditions look stable. Local advice required.

### Day 5 — recovery, return
- Recovery in Chamonix. Pizzas, beer.
- Fly out (or chain more climbs — [[#Adjacent objectives|see below]])

## Key gear (highlights — full list in [[Mont Blanc Gear List]])

- **Double mountaineering boots** with vertical-front crampon compatibility (La Sportiva Nepal Cube, Scarpa Phantom Tech)
- **Steel mountaineering crampons** — 12-point, anti-balling plates
- **Mountaineering axe** — 50–60 cm walking axe
- **Glacier rope** — 30–40 m, 8–9 mm half-rope is plenty for rope team of 2–3
- **Glacier travel kit** — pickets (1–2), screws (2–3), prussiks, slings, two anchor cams (BD Camalot 0.5 + 1 typical), 60 cm helmet-friendly harness
- **Cold-weather layering** for a 04:00 summit ridge at –15 °C with wind

## Trois Monts–specific risks

> [!warning] Mont Blanc du Tacul serac
> The *Triangle du Tacul* has dropped major serac collapses repeatedly, including the **2008 collapse** that killed 8 climbers. There is no fully safe time, but **early in the morning (cold, frozen)** is statistically safer than afternoon. **Climb the Tacul slope in the dark, descend it in the morning, never linger.**

> [!warning] Maudit slope
> Steep firm snow / ice; some parties protect with a single screw or picket placement on lead. Front-pointing for sustained sections. **If you don't have ice tool / steep snow technique, take a guide.**

> [!warning] Crevasses on Col Maudit
> The flat glacier between Tacul and Maudit has hidden crevasses. Stay on the broken trail or rope-up tightly. **Crevasse rescue drills before the trip are essential.**

> [!warning] Altitude
> 4,808 m without proper acclimatization is genuine altitude. AMS at the summit ridge is common. **One night at Cosmiques + 1–2 prior acclimatization climbs is the minimum.** Going from Chamonix valley to summit in one push is reckless.

## Guided vs unguided
- **Guides are the default** for Trois Monts unguided. The Maudit slope + objective hazard makes IFMGA guidance an entirely reasonable call **even for experienced climbers** on a first Mont Blanc.
- **Compagnie des Guides de Chamonix** — 200+ year institution, premier guide service. ~€2,500–3,200 for guided Trois Monts (2-3 day program, 1:1 or 1:2). [chamonix-guides.com](https://www.chamonix-guides.com/)
- **Argentière Guides Office**, [chamex.com](https://www.chamex.com/), various independent IFMGA guides
- **Unguided is reasonable** with: (a) recent glacier-travel + crevasse-rescue practice; (b) experienced rope partner; (c) prior alpine climbing at this technical grade; (d) acclimatization plan; (e) honest weather window.
- See [[Guides & Operators]] for selection criteria

## Decisions to make

- [ ] **Guided or unguided?** Lean unguided given background, but **strongly consider a single guided acclimatization day** for technique refresh — Cosmiques Arête + Maudit slope tour for ~€500
- [ ] **Acclimatization plan:** Aiguille du Tour overnight + Cosmiques summit push (4 days on mtn) vs straight to Cosmiques (2 days, harder summit)
- [ ] **Window:** mid-June (long days, less crowded, snow still good) vs early September (shorter days, often best weather, less serac stable)
- [ ] **Partner alpine experience:** glacier travel + crevasse rescue + 4,000 m experience required. If new to glacier — guide.

## Pre-trip checklist

- [ ] Refuge des Cosmiques booking confirmed (6 months ahead)
- [ ] Flights DEN-GVA booked, shuttle GVA-Chamonix arranged
- [ ] ÖAV Alpenverein or Global Rescue insurance active
- [ ] Crevasse rescue drill + glacier travel refresh practiced in last 90 days
- [ ] Weather + serac conditions checked at La Chamoniarde 48h before climb
- [ ] PGHM number saved in phone: **+33 4 50 53 16 89**
- [ ] SafeMountain Chamonix app installed
