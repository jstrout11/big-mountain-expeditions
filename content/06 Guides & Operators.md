# Guides & Operators

Vault default is **unguided**, but every objective has guided options worth keeping on the bench. Also relevant for **skills days** (Teton) and **operators that handle non-guided air taxi / logistics** (Denali).

## Grand Teton

### Exum Mountain Guides
- **The** Teton guide service. Co-founded by Glenn Exum (Exum Ridge first ascent).
- Permanent base hut at Lower Saddle.
- **Programs:**
  - Guided Owen-Spalding 2-day: $1,200–1,500/person (2:1 typical)
  - Guided Exum Ridge: similar pricing
  - **Single-day skills clinics:** $600/day for 2:1 — anchor building, rappel, short-rope technique. **Excellent prep value before Mont Blanc / Denali.**
  - 4-day technical climbing courses
- [exumguides.com](https://www.exumguides.com/)

### Jackson Hole Mountain Guides
- Second major outfit, equally reputable
- Often better availability than Exum on short notice
- Wider non-Grand programs (skiing, ice climbing, climbing schools)
- [jhmg.com](https://www.jhmg.com/)

### Decision framework
- **Doing OS unguided?** No guide needed.
- **Want a skills refresh pre-Mont Blanc?** Single Exum or JHMG skills day, ~$600. Great value.
- **Have a less-experienced partner?** Hire Exum to guide them, you walk along (cheaper alternative to fully guiding both).

## Mont Blanc / Chamonix

### Compagnie des Guides de Chamonix
- The oldest mountain guides company in the world (founded 1821).
- IFMGA-certified guides only.
- **Programs:**
  - Guided Mont Blanc Trois Monts: 2-3 day program, €2,500–3,200 (1:1 or 1:2)
  - Mont Blanc preparation course: 4-day program, €2,000–2,500 — includes Aiguille du Tour + Cosmiques climb + Trois Monts
  - Single-day acclimatization / skills with guide: €400–600
- [chamonix-guides.com](https://www.chamonix-guides.com/)

### Chamex
- Aggregator + independent guides
- Often easier to find English-speaking guide with availability
- [chamex.com](https://www.chamex.com/)

### Argentière Guides Office
- Smaller, valley-based collective in Argentière (5 km north of Chamonix)
- IFMGA guides at competitive rates
- [guides-argentiere.com](https://www.guides-argentiere.com/)

### Other notable European operators
- **Adventure Consultants (NZ-based, runs Mont Blanc programs)**: [adventureconsultants.com](https://www.adventureconsultants.com/)
- **Alpenglow Expeditions** (US, runs Alps trips): [alpenglowexpeditions.com](https://www.alpenglowexpeditions.com/)

### Decision framework
- **Doing Trois Monts unguided?** Reasonable with prior glacier experience. Strongly consider a **1-day pre-climb skills/acclimatization day with a Chamonix guide** for ~€500 — best value-add of any guide service across the three trips.
- **First glacier trip / first major alpine route?** Guide the whole thing. €2,500–3,200 is fair for the safety margin.
- **Solo (no partner)?** Hire a 1:1 guide or join a small group from one of the operators above.

## Denali / Alaska Range

### The "big four" Denali guide services
NPS approves a small set of operators for Denali. The standard four:

| Operator | Style | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **American Alpine Institute (AAI)** | Strong skills focus, smaller groups | $11,500–13,000 | Bellingham WA-based; great prep programs |
| **Alpine Ascents International** | Premium, more services | $12,500–14,500 | Seattle-based |
| **Mountain Trip** | Ouray-based, classic-style | $11,500–12,500 | Long-running, well-regarded |
| **RMI Expeditions** | Largest, structured | $12,500–14,000 | Ashford WA-based; runs Rainier programs as feeder |

- All offer 21-day West Buttress programs in May/June
- 1:3 to 1:4 guide:client ratios typical
- Includes all permit fees, air taxi, group gear, group food
- Excludes personal gear, flights to Anchorage, hotels, tips

### Decision framework
- **Have a strong rope team + Alaska experience?** Go unguided. Save $8,000–10,000.
- **No rope team or first major expedition?** Go guided. Worth every dollar for the safety + skills layer.
- **Mixed experience team?** Some teams hire **a private guide** for a self-organized group (~$12,000–15,000 for guide + share group gear costs). Custom but flexible.

### Air taxi operators (for unguided teams)
- **Talkeetna Air Taxi (TAT)** — Otters and Beavers. The standard. [talkeetnaair.com](https://www.talkeetnaair.com/)
- **K2 Aviation** — newer, comparable service. [flyk2.com](https://flyk2.com/)
- **Sheldon Air Service** — smaller, family-owned. [sheldonairservice.com](https://sheldonairservice.com/)
- **Cost:** $700–900/person round trip with climbing gear allowance
- **Reservations:** book 4–6 months out for May/June

### Talkeetna ground services
- **Walter Harper Talkeetna Ranger Station** — mandatory check-in, not a service per se
- **Talkeetna Roadhouse** — historic climbing lodge, books fast
- **AMH Anchorage** — gear shop, rental, fuel

## How to evaluate any guide service

- ✅ **IFMGA / AMGA certified guides** — non-negotiable for technical objectives
- ✅ **Liability insurance + permit compliance** — every NPS-approved Denali outfit is fine; Alps less consistent
- ✅ **Guide-to-client ratio appropriate to objective** — 1:1 or 1:2 for technical, 1:3 max for non-technical
- ✅ **Recent successful trip reports / reviews** — Mountain Project, TripAdvisor, AAC forums
- ✅ **Conservative weather decision-making track record** — ask about turn-around stories
- ✅ **Clear gear / fitness expectations communicated in advance**
- ⚠️ **Beware:** newer outfits without IFMGA certification, "value" pricing well below competitors, vague cancellation policies

## Tipping conventions

| Service | Tip |
|---|---|
| Single-day guide (Teton, Chamonix skills) | 10–15% of fee |
| Multi-day guided climb (Mont Blanc) | 5–10% of fee |
| Denali guided trip | $500–1,500 total to be split among guides ($800 typical) |
| Air taxi pilot (Talkeetna) | $50–100 if flight in + out went smoothly |
